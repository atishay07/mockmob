# MockMob generator: quality-only assessment

Prepared 8 October 2026 after campaign C completed. Owner requested 15–20 criteria focused on CUET readiness, with cost excluded. This assessment uses 20 criteria. It changes no generation prompt, academic gate, frozen outcome, publication state or accounting. Further optimization remains held.

**My overall judgment is about 6/10 for the latest measured question outputs.** There are sound, useful CUET practice questions, especially numericals. The output is uneven in learning value, difficulty calibration, distractors and semantic novelty. It is not yet a consistently premium CUET generator suitable for unattended student publication. This is a reasoned assessment, not a measured accuracy percentage, a claim that every question is mediocre, or a release decision.

The completed live campaign used the older v7.2 validator. The current v7.4 author/validator software is implemented, but its live question quality is unmeasured and its academic release is not qualified. I cannot transfer C's results to v7.4 or give v7.4 a supposedly measured quality score.

## Evidence and limitations

C retains all 100 preregistered candidates: 84 machine-eligible, 16 rejected, zero pending, all accepted provider processing complete. New publication count is zero. The final frozen all-candidates SHA256 is `4b06df8aab6c503dc293d6d4c8e47cc0039d396ff1ecfa840c3f98b8f84cf582`; batch-report SHA256 is `a7349592ba60c144c4006d1cf4cefc8f6399f71ce29758d5f83b6a4dc18975d3`.

I directly inspected 20 purposively selected eligible questions, including all five scored 7 and all three labelled hard, plus all three children of the surviving English passage group. Review covered stems, options, stored answers, explanations and supporting source material. This was nonblinded and used previously evaluated examples. It is diagnostic review, not fresh independent reliability evidence, a random sample or an exhaustive accuracy audit of all 84. Export/source-reference integrity checks cover the full denominator but do not prove every explanation follows from its source.

The scores below combine observed output strengths and weaknesses with the strength of existing validation evidence. They do not estimate the percentage of correct answers. Rough interpretation: 8 means solid observed strength with limits; 6 means functional but inconsistent or insufficiently assured; 4 means a material demonstrated weakness. Easy questions can be excellent CUET practice: difficulty is not quality, and gratuitous complexity earns no credit. No average can compensate for a wrong key, multiple answers, missing decisive assumptions, unsupported claims, syllabus mismatch or broken numerical.

## Concrete contrast: useful CUET practice versus weaker fit

**Strong Accountancy example — `41bdeb67f4f7ef1bed646c7637804117`.** A dissolved firm realises ₹2,00,000, pays ₹80,000 outside liabilities, and owes partner advances P ₹90,000 and Q ₹60,000. Its ₹1,20,000 balance repays advances proportionately, so P receives ₹72,000. The ₹96,000 distractor applies the irrelevant 4:1 profit ratio. This tests an actual within-syllabus accounting distinction, supplies decisive conditions, and has a source-supported derivation and meaningful distractors. It is good medium-level CUET practice without needing an authenticated past-paper counterpart.

**Weak objective alignment — `f1d2a614a08057702839bbab5cf72b1b`.** Stored score 9, labelled hard, concept `english.vocabulary.sceptical`. It matches explicitly stated doubt/certainty/no-view positions to virtually equivalent descriptions. The word sceptical never appears. Its key can be correct and its literal reading coherent while it fails to test the vocabulary objective claimed by its metadata. It should not be presented as a strong hard vocabulary item. This finding does not establish that every possible literal-matching English task is outside the syllabus.

**Weak domain demand — `21becfa473f5c0f8b609fe3769c2becc`.** Stored score 9, labelled hard, Business Studies ordering. “That grouping”, “This emphasis” and “Following this occupational specialisation” reveal the sequence. Some learning value remains, but the difficulty is overstated and a learner can solve much of it through sentence references rather than Organising knowledge.

**Answer-cued Economics — `31f50e5123b09da55650472d1a55d36c`.** The opening sentence supplies the WTO objective; two statements repeat it, while the others deny rules or replace world resources with domestic-only resources. The key and quoted support are sound. Its weakness is preparation value for subject recall/application, not a claimed wrong answer.

**Inflated hard Accountancy — `e47679b2c01f3777ef9b29d7b124f52c`.** The statement-selection task contrasts reasonable financial-statement uses with “guarantee every decision” and “only shareholders”. Those conspicuous absolutes make a correct easy item appear harder in its metadata than its actual demand.

The sound common-size example `b7be101284d349b74625e1f006c7644c` and debenture-value example `1e8d77c5ca2b35b652cf04eff792992e` further show that the generator can produce useful subject-specific numericals. Simple valid direct questions are also valuable and should not be rejected merely for being easy.

## What “software finished” does and does not mean

The explanation checker, independent-validator adapters, persistence, budget protection, current-profile binding and atomic publication route are implemented. Latest saved local checks are factory 570/570, recovery 40/40 and question/payment/wallet 71/71; build and legacy staging checks also passed. Those show that the software runs and fails closed. They do not show that its judgments reliably represent CUET demand or catch every wrong explanation.

Fresh v7.3 retained only 12/22 designated valid controls and rejected 19/19 controlled defects. It failed its preregistered qualification; this does not justify weakening correctness gates. Current v7.4's historical diagnostic is regression-only. Its new known-critical requests failed in transport and provide no new academic judgments. Current independent reference answers, selected official-key comparison and fresh qualification remain unfinished. Current-profile staging publication/restart/retrieval/dispute evidence is also incomplete.

**Unattended operation:** persisted candidate-processing/quarantine infrastructure is built, but current v7.4 dispatch/publication remains release-blocked. It is not ready for an unattended student-facing publishing run. The intended final product should need no routine human review and no agent watching every batch. It still needs one genuine current qualification and completed staging proof before that claim is defensible. Handing off the work is sensible; calling the generator perfected or released is not.

Future improvements, only after the owner resumes work: require a specific subject learning objective in generation and judge whether the final task actually tests it; calibrate difficulty against authenticated exam references rather than trusting labels; evaluate distractors as plausible subject misconceptions; deduplicate reasoning/idea and stimulus reuse rather than only exact text; preserve blind correctness/source gates; complete independent current qualification and staging proof. None of these changes was made for this assessment.
