# CBSE 2025–26 additional candidate custody

This is an isolated, private construction set derived from CBSE Class XII sample question papers and their official marking schemes. These are **not CUET PYQs**, are not yet independently answer-reviewed, and are not approved benchmark positives. The four added PDF families improve documentary-key coverage while retaining strong within-paper, within-subject and shared-syllabus correlation.

The question-only file is `questions-blinded-2025-26-v1.json`. Answers and key locators are in the separate sealed custody sidecar `official-key-custody-2025-26-v1.sealed.json`; do not expose it to blind reviewers until their first-pass answers are frozen. Exact PDFs, URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes are in `source-manifest-2025-26.json`.

The English source passages were shortened and paraphrased to create stimuli below 300 words; their questions therefore require independent rederivation against the paraphrases. Two Economics candidates are CBSE’s visually impaired alternative forms, not the standard diagram/data forms. English Q2-II choices were unlabelled in the source; A/B/C labels were added solely for machine-readable custody. Official answer-key agreement alone does not establish CUET syllabus fit or question readiness.

The downloaded PDFs are retained for private audit. Do not redistribute full papers or represent these items as authentic CUET questions. The sealed sidecar is separated by file and is not encrypted. No provider calls, benchmark judgments, publication or release changes were made.
