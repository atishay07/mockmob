Under that curriculum convention, Statement 1 is true and the official key C is supported; the reviewer appears to have applied a broader RBI series definition including other deposits with RBI. This is convention-sensitive if the stem is read as an exhaustive statement about every RBI-published measure rather than the NCERT syllabus definition.

Suggested repair: Keep the official CBSE key for a CUET/NCERT-aligned review; cite the current NCERT convention in the evidence record. If precision is desired, word Statement 1 as “For this chapter’s measure, M1 = currency held by the public plus net demand deposits with commercial banks.”
