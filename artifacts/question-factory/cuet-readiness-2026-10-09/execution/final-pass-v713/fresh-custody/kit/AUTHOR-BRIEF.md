# V7.13 fresh source kit (pre-freeze)

This folder contains source evidence and the blinded-review/authoring specification only. It contains no authored questions, options, answer keys, explanations, defect constructions, or reviewer judgments. The V7.13 contract is not frozen; this kit does not establish a start time or authorize authoring. Do not use it to begin the later sealed-case phase until the parent confirms the exact contract freeze.

`source-fact-kits.json` has 9 source slots per subject: five planned valid-item objectives and four source bases reserved for later controlled defects. Each source fact is bound to its active registry id/version/locator/support hash, exact excerpt, checked extraction, and underlying file identity. Accountancy uses the Cash Flow Statement chapter; Business Studies uses Planning and Marketing; Economics uses National Income Accounting. English uses nine verified, reusable Oxford lexical entries that do not repeat the exposed q1/q2/q3/pilot target words recorded in the prep audit.

`official-syllabus-slices.json` includes exact slices from the active CUET (UG) 2026 syllabus records, with official PDF and extraction identities, registered full-syllabus reference, and an independent digest of each slice. These are entitlement boundaries for the source objectives, not claims that the syllabus is a 2027 final edition.

`blinded-review-schema.json` reflects the current `freshCustodyV713.mjs` contract. Eight separately hashed artifacts are required: authored items, opaque-id map, key-free blind packet, blind output, pre-reveal blind freeze, exact reveal, explanation audit, and novelty report. The map and reveal each contain the exact same ordered 36 answer rows. Roles must be independent. The novelty report compares semantic objectives and solution paths against all hash-custodied prior history, not just source-reference overlap. The blind packet contains the exact candidate body/options and supplied source/syllabus evidence, but no candidate id, answer, explanation, group, or defect label. The sealed map has exactly 36 rows of `{fixture_id, opaque_id, content_hash, correct_answer, expected_valid}`; the reveal must contain those exact same rows in the same order and is opened only after blind freeze. Reviewers derive all four option judgments and their key from the key-free packet before the blind output is frozen. Only after that freeze are keys and author-side labels revealed for the separate explanation/defect audit.

## Later authoring constraints

After the contract freeze only, prepare 36 fixed fixtures: five valid and four genuinely defective per subject. The 20 positive controls must have a source-grounded unique key, supported complete explanation, decisive printed assumptions, valid format/presentation, and explicit CUET syllabus entitlement. Defect fixtures must each contain one independently confirmed defect with concrete decisive evidence; do not use source silence, a speculative counterexample, missing status that the stem does not need, incidental wording, or an out-of-syllabus positive as a defect. If a claimed missing input matters, show two reasonable realizations preserving every printed fact and rule that produce different keys. Keep every fixture in the fixed denominator, with no post-hoc exclusion or replacement.

Every candidate needs a distinct objective/family identity, full source references, exact supplied excerpts, official syllabus slice, and a concise explanation grounded in the supplied evidence. Preserve decisive assumptions and unique-key support. For each option, make the whole option true or decisively false under all printed facts; true fragments do not rescue false conjunctions. No item may depend on a textbook illustration silently supplying a case fact. Avoid q1/q2/q3 and pilot semantic ideas, including near-paraphrases and changed-number variants; record semantic novelty against hash-custodied history.

Difficulty is independently assessed after correctness gates. Easy items should be valid, useful warm-up practice. Medium means normal CUET format and cognitive demand: an entitled concept application, short-passage inference, plausible distinction between syllabus concepts, or modest linked reasoning. A direct definition lookup, obvious lexical-root match, one-step warm-up, or value-changed copy is not medium. For English, do not fill the quota with trivial standalone lexical-root/definition items; use context-rich word choice, passage comprehension, ordering, or matching at ordinary CUET demand, with enough supplied language evidence to make the task answerable. Hard is optional and should be used only for demand materially above the usual CUET level while staying within the syllabus and time budget. Length, obscurity, confusing wording, and arithmetic alone do not make an item hard. Use authenticated subject/format examples where available; otherwise report the basis as a heuristic.

When the later contract is frozen, preserve its exact decision rule: zero controlled false accepts, at least 85% pooled valid survival, and at least 70% valid survival in each subject. For 20 valid controls this requires at least 17 pooled and at least 4 in each subject. Report small-sample limits: zero accepted defects among 16 controls does not establish a negligible real-world false-accept rate, and four-per-subject survival is coarse evidence. No result from the source kit is qualification or publication evidence.
