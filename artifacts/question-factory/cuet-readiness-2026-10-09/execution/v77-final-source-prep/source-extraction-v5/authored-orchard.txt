Leela’s first harvest came in uneven batches. Rather than send every basket to market at once, she asked buyers to choose a collection window and wrote each promise beside the buyer’s name. The first week, one buyer arrived early. Leela did not scold him; she showed him the list and offered the next available slot. By the end of the month, buyers had begun checking the list before setting out. Leela kept the system because it made small delays visible while there was still time to adjust.
