When the village clock stopped, Arun carried it to the repair room and placed its screws in a shallow tray. His apprentice wanted to replace the whole mechanism immediately. Arun first asked her to compare the movement of the second hand with the sound of the escapement. They found a thread caught near one gear. After removing it, they watched the clock for an hour before declaring the repair complete. The apprentice later said the waiting had taught her as much as the repair.
