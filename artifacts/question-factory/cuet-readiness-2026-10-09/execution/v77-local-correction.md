## Narrow valid-case corrections

Root review found two conservative over-rejections. An explicitly arithmetic audit may now use empty evidence IDs when all calculation premises are exact substrings of the stem; the parent source/provenance and academic gates still apply. A false conjunction may also retain an accurately unresolved, nondecisive clause when the whole option and at least one separate contradictory clause are entailed from direct stem/source evidence or explicit arithmetic. Unresolved nonchosen full options still fail; source silence remains uncertainty and requires trusted selected context IDs, while any quoted premises must match the stem/source. The explanation prompt now distinguishes this permitted uncertainty from decisive unresolved alternatives, and clarifies that the Luna explanation-stage auditor sees both matrices only after independent blind derivation; the independently derived keys are visible at that later audit stage, while initial blind payloads retain their key boundary.

`verifyV77` now validates fresh adapter identity/mock status before its outer stage-cache write, leaving invalid cached entries untouched and failing closed. Offline full-pipeline controls cover a false conjunction with a contradicted clause plus a nondecisive unknown, arithmetic based solely on explicit stem premises, and a still-rejected unresolved alternative.
