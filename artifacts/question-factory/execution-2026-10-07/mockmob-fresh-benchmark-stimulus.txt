Asha’s class planned a visit to the old observatory. The first bus was cancelled because its driver was ill. The teacher booked another bus and moved the departure from eight o’clock to nine. Asha complained that they would miss the morning demonstration. The observatory manager agreed to repeat it in the afternoon, but asked the class to spend the morning examining the exhibition instead. When Asha returned, she said the unexpected change had given her more time to study the models before seeing them work.
