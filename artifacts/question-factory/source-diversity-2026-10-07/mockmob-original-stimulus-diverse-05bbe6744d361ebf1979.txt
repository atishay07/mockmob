Meera unfolded a paper boat her brother had left on the windowsill. Beneath the creases she found the drawing they had used to make it: a house, two crooked trees and a sun almost hidden at the edge. She considered flattening the sheet completely, but the folds resisted. Instead, she opened only enough to see the drawing and set it beside a photograph. The boat no longer stood upright. The picture was not smooth either. In the evening light, both forms remained visible, neither restored exactly to what it had been before.
