A fictional garden committee published a watering roster on a noticeboard. When a volunteer could not attend, that person was asked to arrange a replacement and update the board. After two missed sessions, the committee learned that replacements had been arranged but the board had not been changed. Other volunteers therefore believed the slots were empty and duplicated the work. The committee added a simple confirmation step: the replacement would initial the revised slot. The adjustment addressed the recording of responsibility rather than imposing an additional watering session. The report did not attribute the duplication to volunteers being unwilling to help.
