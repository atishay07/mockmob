Rohan volunteered to carry a box of costumes to the school hall. On the way, he saw a younger pupil looking for a lost notebook. Rohan could not search while holding the large box, and he knew the costumes were needed soon. He took the box directly to the hall and asked another volunteer to help the pupil search. When he returned, the notebook had been found beneath a bench. The pupil thanked both of them. Rohan had initially felt that leaving the search meant refusing to help. Seeing the result, he realised that asking the right person could be useful assistance too.
