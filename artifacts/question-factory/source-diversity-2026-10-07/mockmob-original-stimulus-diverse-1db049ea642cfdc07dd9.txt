A fictional community centre offered a beginner's pottery workshop and a more advanced workshop. Registration for the advanced session required completion of the beginner's session or a short demonstration of equivalent skills. An experienced visitor who had never attended the centre passed the demonstration and joined the advanced group. Another visitor chose the beginner's session after finding the demonstration difficult. The organiser explained that the alternative routes recognised prior experience while checking readiness for the work. Attending a particular earlier course was one way to qualify, rather than the only acceptable evidence of the relevant skills.
