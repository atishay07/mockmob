Ishaan was preparing labels for a class display when a classmate suggested including a small model built by the quietest pupil. There was no space left on the table. Ishaan initially proposed displaying only its photograph. After looking again, he moved two large labels onto the wall and made room for the model. Its maker demonstrated a moving part that the photograph would not have shown. Ishaan kept the photograph beside it to show an earlier version. He had not removed another pupil's work; he had changed the arrangement so the display could communicate more of what was already there.
