Sana's grandmother asked her to translate a letter aloud. One sentence could mean either that a visit had been postponed or that its timing was still undecided. Sana first chose the interpretation she thought her grandmother would prefer. Then she paused, read the sentence again and explained both possibilities. Her grandmother suggested calling the writer rather than guessing. The call confirmed that no date had yet been fixed. Sana wrote that information beside the letter. She had wanted to give a reassuring answer immediately; instead, the uncertainty became a question they could resolve together.
