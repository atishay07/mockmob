Kabir built a shelf for his younger sister's books. He carefully measured the wall and made the shelf fit its empty space. When he invited her to use it, she struggled to reach the upper level. Kabir first suggested that she stand on a stool, then stopped when she asked why a shelf intended for her had to be so high. He removed the brackets and lowered the shelf. A strip of unpainted wall remained above it. He decided to leave the painting until the weekend. That evening, his sister arranged her books without help, and Kabir was pleased despite the unfinished wall.
