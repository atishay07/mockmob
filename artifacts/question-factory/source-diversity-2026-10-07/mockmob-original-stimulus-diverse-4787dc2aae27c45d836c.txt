At a rehearsal, Nisha played a melody more quickly than the rest of the group. She thought the faster pace made it exciting. Their conductor asked everyone to listen while one drummer kept a steady beat. Nisha noticed that the flute's quieter phrases disappeared when she rushed. During the next attempt, she waited for the drummer's beat and heard the phrases clearly. Afterwards, a classmate praised the performance. Nisha said that she had changed very few notes. What she had changed was the space she gave the other players. She kept her faster version for a solo rehearsal later.
