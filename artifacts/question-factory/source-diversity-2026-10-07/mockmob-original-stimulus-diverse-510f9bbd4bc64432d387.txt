Leela was asked to announce the winner of the school poster competition. She recognised her friend's poster among the finalists and wanted it to win. Before the judges arrived, she noticed that its entry number was missing. Instead of attaching her friend's number from memory, she asked the coordinator to check the registration sheet. The check revealed that two posters had been placed in the wrong slots. They were moved before judging began. Her friend thanked her for helping. Leela replied that she had helped the entries be judged as they were submitted. She would have asked for the same check if the misplaced poster had belonged to someone else.
