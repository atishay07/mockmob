For years, the brass bell above the shop door had announced every visitor. When it cracked, the shopkeeper fitted a quieter bell. His daughter liked being able to hear a conversation without interruption. He still turned towards the door before the new bell rang, expecting the old sound. One evening, he placed the cracked bell beside the till rather than returning it to the doorway. A customer lifted it gently and recognised its worn handle. The shopkeeper smiled. The old bell could remain part of the shop without being required to do its former work.
