In a fictional school, a bus timetable was revised after students reported long waits. The school shifted one departure from 4:00 to 4:15, allowing pupils in a late laboratory session to reach it. Pupils whose classes ended earlier consequently waited longer. The school retained an earlier departure on another route and asked students to choose the departure suited to their class schedule. The notice described the revision as a compromise between different finishing times. It neither promised shorter waits for everyone nor described the later departure as a faster journey.
