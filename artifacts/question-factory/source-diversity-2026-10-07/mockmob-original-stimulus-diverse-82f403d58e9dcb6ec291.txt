In this fictional report, a school canteen tested two ways of reducing disposable cups. During the first week, a sign asked students to bring their own cups. During the second, the canteen also lent washable cups against a refundable deposit. Disposable-cup use fell in both weeks, with a larger fall in the second. However, attendance was lower during the second week because one class was away. The report therefore recommended comparing cups used per student before attributing the larger fall to the lending scheme. It also recorded that most borrowed cups were returned. The trial suggested a possible benefit, while leaving the size of that benefit uncertain.
