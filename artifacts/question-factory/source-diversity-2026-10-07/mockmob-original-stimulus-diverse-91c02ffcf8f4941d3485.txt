Dev kept score during a friendly cricket match. As the players packed their bags, he noticed that he had counted one run twice. Correcting it would turn his team's victory into a tie. He showed the scorebook to the captains, who checked the recorded deliveries and agreed on the correction. One teammate said nobody would have noticed. Dev answered that noticing it himself was enough reason to check. The captains changed the result without replaying the match. On the walk home, Dev felt disappointed about the lost victory but relieved that the scorebook now matched the game.
