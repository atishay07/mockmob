The lamp in the study cast a circle across the desk. Outside that circle, the shelves looked almost empty, though they held the same books as always. Arjun moved the lamp closer to a page he was struggling to read. The circle tightened, and the rest of the room grew dimmer. After finishing the paragraph, he raised the shade and saw the shelves again. He had changed where the light fell, not what the room contained. He left the lamp slightly higher while choosing his next book.
