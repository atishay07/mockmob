The painter covered a wall with a pale morning sky. Before she could finish the birds, evening sunlight turned the colours warm. She stepped back, displeased that the light had changed what she had planned. A boy standing nearby said the unfinished shapes looked like leaves caught in a breeze. The painter looked again. She did not erase the sky or declare the boy correct. She sketched one clear bird among the remaining shapes and returned the next morning to see the wall in the light for which she had first designed it.
