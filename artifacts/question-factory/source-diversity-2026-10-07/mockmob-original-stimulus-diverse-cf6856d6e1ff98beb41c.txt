The old tailor kept a drawer of buttons. Some were pairs; most had lost their companions long ago. When his niece offered to throw away the unmatched ones, he spread them across the counter. There was a small blue button from her first school shirt. She recognised it by its chipped rim, although she could no longer remember losing it. The tailor did not ask her to keep the whole drawer. He handed her the blue button, and together they sorted the rest. By evening, the drawer was lighter, but it had not become empty.
