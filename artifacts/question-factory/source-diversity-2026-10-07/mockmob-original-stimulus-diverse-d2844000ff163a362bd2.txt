A path crossed the field where Anaya's grandfather had walked every evening. After he moved away, the grass began to cover its edges. Anaya followed it once, trying to place her feet exactly where his had been. At the far fence she saw another narrow track leading towards a new gate. She took that route home. From the window, the two paths looked like lines beginning together and separating near the fence. She wrote to her grandfather that the field had not lost his route; it had made room for another one beside it.
