A fictional repair club allocated its weekly budget to tools and replacement parts. Members initially proposed buying a new drill. The inventory check then showed that an existing drill worked but lacked a charging cable, while several repairs were waiting for inexpensive switches. The club bought the cable and switches instead. This allowed it to resume the waiting repairs without exhausting the budget. The minutes explained that the decision concerned the club's immediate needs; they did not claim that buying new equipment was always wasteful. Members agreed to reconsider the drill purchase if the repaired equipment could no longer handle their workload.
