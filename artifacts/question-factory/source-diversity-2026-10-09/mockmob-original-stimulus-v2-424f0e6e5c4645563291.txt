Had the apples vanished from the crates in Brackett's orchard, or had they simply been packed into the wrong baskets? One explanation was theft by a passing worker, while the other was careless sorting by pickers who worked in a hurry. The stronger evidence favoured the latter view, because every missing apple turned up within a few feet of its proper crate and no gate had been forced. The pickers, not the passing workers, were therefore responsible, and the orchard asks each picker to label every basket they fill.
