Staff at Harbin Lane school noticed that lost jumpers collected far more often in the box beside the sports field gate than in the box by the library. They suspected that the gate box simply sat in the path of the crowd leaving the field, so the placement might explain the difference. To test this, the staff swapped the two boxes and counted the jumpers that reached each one. Lost jumpers collected in the library box instead, which confirmed that position, rather than the carelessness of pupils, had decided where things were left.
