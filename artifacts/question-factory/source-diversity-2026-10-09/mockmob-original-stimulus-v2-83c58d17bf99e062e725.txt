Consider how a rumour travels through a street as if it were water moving through a network of pipes. Each house is a junction where the flow collects and redirects, so the people who hear a rumour most often become the places where it pools and spreads further. The comparison breaks down, however, because water arrives unchanged, whereas each listener adds a little colour to the story and passes it on. The practical lesson is that a correction must reach the houses where a rumour has been reshaped, not merely the place where it began.
