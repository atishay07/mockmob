A keepsake is an ordinary object that its owner keeps because of the memories attached to it rather than for any practical purpose. Keepsakes fall into two groups: inherited objects that arrive through family without any decision, and chosen objects that the owner gathers deliberately. A cracked teacup handed down from grandmother to granddaughter belongs to the inherited group, since nobody picked it out yet nobody could bear to discard it. By contrast, a pebble picked up on a windy beach belongs to the chosen group, because its finder decided it was worth carrying home.
