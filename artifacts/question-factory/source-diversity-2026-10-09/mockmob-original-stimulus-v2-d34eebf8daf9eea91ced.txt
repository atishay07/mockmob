Heavy rain washed out the cart road to Fenwick, so traders had to send their goods by slow barge along the river. The barge trip took twice as long, so travellers who would have broken their journey at the Crown inn chose to bypass it entirely. Without those guests, Mistress Ruddock could not afford her stable boys and the stables fell into disrepair. In reply, she painted a sign for the riverside landing offering hot meals to bargemen, and the inn regained much of its trade.
