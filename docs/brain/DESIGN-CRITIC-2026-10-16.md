# Design critic, 16 Oct 2026 (read-only pass, no code touched)

Judge: the owner's bar in `mockmob-design-direction`, `web-restructure-rejected` and CTO-LOOP section 1 (premium like HIGH, Revolut and Fampay; Duolingo-level character; balanced; one spacing system; no boxes inside boxes; one primary action per screen; Night and Day parity; nothing covered or clipped; real numbers with denominators; Mobi at most once per screen; plain copy; 48 dp targets).

What I looked at: the newest masters in `wt-phase2/flutter_app/test/golden/**` (384 x 853 dp frames, Night and Day pairs) for onboarding, Today, Practice, the Start sheet and quick-start, the runner (practice, mock, NTA, submit, answer sheet), the recap and full result, Review, repair, Your rules, Revise, Insights and Ask, You hub and the account sheet, Credits, Appearance, Help, tools (Compass, Combo, guide), Learn, Rhythm, Milestones, Notifications, sign-in, empty states and the tab bar. I opened about 120 of the 1,015 masters as Night and Day contact sheets. Every Night golden has a Day twin. Day and Night are pixel-identical only for the welcome scene, the splash, the cold open and the NTA skin (NTA is exam-faithful on purpose).

Verdict in one line: the app is already rich, warm and alive, and Day and Night are consistent. What a student will notice first is not missing richness but (1) the Today hero doing too much, (2) text and buttons colliding at the bottom of scrolling screens, (3) the same few components rebuilt four ways, (4) fine print and statistics that talk like a lawyer, and (5) the quieter screens (Learn, runner, Rules, Review) that have none of the character the front screens have. Nothing below asks to cut a section: each fix reorders, tightens, relabels or layers.

Ranking is by how much a student would notice. Groups at the end are builder packets with disjoint paths. Paths are under `wt-phase2/flutter_app/lib/`. The current-owner agents hold `features/you/**`, `rules/**`, `result/**`, `review/**`, `today/**`, `core/mascot/**`, `core/widgets/bar/**` and `core/theme/**`; packets touching them must wait for those agents to land.

## Method limits

Read-only. I judged static frames: motion, haptics and reduced-motion behaviour are not visible in goldens. Fixture text such as "Illustrative revise question 5" and the mismatched credit balances across screens (24/18, 38/8, 40/9) come from test fixtures, not product copy, and are not counted above. The `failures/` folders were ignored.
