# Reference decisions

Owner request: use taste and publicly accessible design resources, without Mobbin Pro.
Reviewed on October 1, 2026. Use reference patterns, never competitor assets or claims.

## Public search space
- [Landing Gallery](https://www.landing.gallery/): page advertised 1,786+ examples when inspected. Free visible gallery and pricing/Next.js filters.
- [One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/): public curated landing pages and templates.
- [Nicepage design library](https://nicepage.com/website-design): advertises 15,000+ design ideas. Breadth reference, not a mandate to use templates.
- [Refero public previews](https://refero.design/websites/106): limited preview screens; logged-in/paid inventory was not accessed.

These catalogs provide a search space above 1,000 references. This is not a claim
that 1,000 individual screens were inspected. Focused inspection and a coherent
design decision are more useful than inventing a large review count.

## Criteria
Persuade on the homepage; Operate in practice and recovery. Start with the promise,
then show an honest interactive example and diagnostic action. Practice content
becomes available only after evidence gates. Use no performance chart without data.
Replay describes observed device events. Recommendations explicitly remain inferred.

Mobile: readable 360px layout, 44px+ targets, no horizontal overflow, five destinations,
visible pending synchronization, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support. Reference
quality does not establish conversion improvement; measure the diagnostic funnel.

## 2 October 2026 — reference pass for the Arena, PrepOS, pricing and landing refinement

Owner asked for 300 to 400 sites. That count was NOT reached and is not claimed. Pages actually fetched and
read (20 requested, 16 returned usable text; 4 blocked or empty): Brilliant, Linear, Khan Academy, Physics
Wallah, Unacademy, Allen, Coursera, Prepium, Cursor pricing, Grammarly plans, Replit pricing, Notion pricing,
Vercel pricing, Stripe, Superhuman, Math Academy. (Duolingo, Quizlet, Perplexity and LeetCode returned no
usable content.) Fetched text describes structure and copy, not pixels; visual judgment came from the
product's own design system and the in-browser checks recorded in STATUS.md.

| Pattern seen | Where | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Show the real workflow with product context, step by step | Linear, Superhuman | Mistake lab: four steps on one labelled example session |
| Diagnose first, then explain the method in plain words | Math Academy, Brilliant | Replay -> leak -> one next move -> later check |
| Price stated as a monthly figure, allowances as plain numbers | Cursor, Grammarly, Notion | Pro shown as one price; PrepOS meter shows "38 of 50 left" |
| Limits and billing detail in FAQ, not on the card | Cursor, Vercel | Pricing FAQ covers renewal, cancel and one-time buyers |
| Indian prep platforms hide price and lead with rank claims | PW, Unacademy, Allen | Show the price openly; use no rank, count or testimonial claims |
| One recommended plan, "free" as an equal first-class entry | Coursera, Notion | Free and Pro cards, Pro featured |
Not adopted: social-proof headcounts, rank claims, urgency timers, "AI" sparkle iconography.
