# NTA engine run on production data

Generated by `scripts/nta-engine-run.mjs` on 2026-10-13 against the production project `isrxrxzjocewrdureyhp`, read-only (select queries only; no session, no shortfall row and no model call). Engine `nta-engine-2026.10.13-r5`, blueprints `2026.10.13-1`. Score bar: mean judge score 8 or more out of 10 (`NTA_MIN_SCORE` in `data/nta_engine.js`). A paper below 50 questions is served only from 45.

Each subject gets three papers for one simulated student: paper 2 leaves out paper 1, paper 3 leaves out papers 1 and 2 (falling back to treating them as seen when the pool is too small). Timings are measured on this machine against the production database; "end to end" covers the pool read, passage, dispute and hold lookups, the gates and assembly.

## Agent pass

Agent scores come from `data/nta_agent_scores/<subject>.json` (merged by `scripts/nta-pass-merge.mjs` from two judges per question, rubric `nta-agent-rubric-1`, `docs/brain/NTA-AGENT-RUBRIC.md`). The engine admits an agent-scored question only when the subject's table is calibrated, both judges are at or above the calibrated threshold, both blind answers equal the stored key, neither judge raised a blocking flag, and the question still has the scored content hash (`data/nta_agent_scores.js`). Factory evidence always takes precedence. Questions where a judge's blind answer differs from the key are listed in `data/nta_key_disputes.json` and kept out whatever their source.
