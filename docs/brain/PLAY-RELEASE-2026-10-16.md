## 1. The owner's steps, in order

The 14-day clock in step 8 is the long pole, so reach it as early as possible.

1. **Google Play developer account.** play.google.com/console, one-time registration fee, identity verification. 2. **Upload key (the main session runs this with him).** Section 2. Back up the keystore and password in two places before anything else.
3. **`prod.json`.** Copy `dart_defines/prod.json.example` to `dart_defines/prod.json`; fill the production Supabase URL, the production anon key (never the service-role key) and the Google **Web** client id (section 3).
4. **Build.** `bash tool/build_release_aab.sh --bump`. Confirm the printed signer is the upload key, not "Android Debug".
5. **Create the app** in the Console: name `MockMob: CUET UG Prep`, default language English (India), App, Free. The package name comes from the first upload and can never change.
6. **Play App Signing.** Leave it on (the default for new apps). Upload the AAB to **Closed testing**, not Production. Play then holds the app signing key; he holds only the upload key.
7. **Copy the app signing fingerprints** (Test and release, App integrity, App signing) into Google Cloud and the server (section 3). Until then Google sign-in on Play installs fails and links open in the browser.
8. **Closed test.** Create a track, add a tester list of **at least 12, aim for 20** (people drop out), send each the opt-in link, and keep them opted in for **14 continuous days**. 11. **Apply for production** after day 14 with 12 or more still opted in; answer the three questionnaires. Google usually replies within about 7 days. Then promote, or upload a new bundle with a higher versionCode.

New uploads during the test: `bash tool/build_release_aab.sh --bump`, upload to the same track. The 14-day count keeps running.

## 2. The keytool command

The main session runs this with the owner. The simple route is the existing script. It prompts for the password (hidden), writes the key outside the repo and `android\key.properties`, and prints both fingerprints:

```powershell
cd "C:\Users\atish\Desktop\mockmob copy\mockmob-next\flutter_app"
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tool\make_upload_key.ps1
```

The command it wraps, for reference (keytool asks for the password on the console when `-storepass` is left off, which keeps it out of shell history):

```powershell
& "C:\Program Files\Android\Android Studio\jbr\bin\keytool.exe" -genkeypair `
  -keystore "$env:USERPROFILE\mockmob-keys\mockmob-upload.jks" -alias upload `
  -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -storetype PKCS12 `
  -dname "CN=MockMob, O=MockMob, C=IN"
```

If run by hand, write `android\key.properties` from `key.properties.example` and read the fingerprints with `keytool -list -v -keystore <path> -alias upload`.

Rules: nobody else sees the password; copy the `.jks` and the password to two separate places; never commit, email or paste them in chat. If the upload key is lost the app is not lost, but replacing it needs a request to Play support and takes days.

## 4. Data safety answers (drafted from the code, 16 Oct)

Method: "collected" means sent off the device; "shared" means given to a third party, not counting service providers that act only for MockMob. Re-checked against `lib` and `pubspec.yaml` on 16 Oct. Changes since the 14 Oct pack: the submit now carries `client` (platform `android`, app version, start time) and `endedBy` (Mistake Lab capture), covered under App interactions; `in_app_update` and `in_app_review` use Google Play's own services and send MockMob nothing.

**Overview**
- Collects data: yes. Shares data: no.
- Encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS only; cleartext is in the debug manifest only).
- Users can request deletion: yes. In the app: You, Account, Delete account. Web: `https://www.mockmob.in/account-deletion`. Account and study records are deleted within 30 days; payment records the law requires are kept; usage data is kept 180 days.
- Delete some data without the account: yes, turning usage data off (You, Privacy) stops collection and deletes earlier usage data.
- Account creation: email (code or password) and Google. Independent security review: no. Families policy: no.

| Data type | Required or optional | Purposes | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | required | App functionality, account management, security | sign-in |
| Name | optional | App functionality, account management | Google sign-in, or typed by the student |
| User IDs | required | App functionality, account management | MockMob and Supabase ids |
| Other personal info (age band) | optional | App functionality, security and compliance | adult, minor or unknown; decides whether usage data may be on |
| App interactions | required | App functionality, personalization, analytics (consented events only) | answers, timing, submit, results, stream views and attempts, lesson events, goal progress, how a paper ended |
| Other user-generated content | optional | App functionality | saves, votes, question reports with a reason label |
| Other actions | optional | App functionality, personalization | weekly goal, pause date, reminder preferences, quiet hours, subjects, timezone |
| Other in-app messages | optional | App functionality, personalization | AI mentor messages, processed for MockMob by the model provider |

Every row: collected yes, shared no, ephemeral no. Not collected, leave unticked: location, financial info, health, photos and videos, audio, files, calendar, contacts, web browsing, installed apps, search history, crash logs, diagnostics, device or other IDs (no advertising id, no push token), phone number. Third parties that touch data, all acting for MockMob: Supabase, Google (sign-in only), Resend (sign-in email), the AI model provider, Vercel. **[CONFIRM]** the AI provider's terms bar training on API data, or the service-provider exemption does not hold. Preview the Data safety card after saving and read it against `/privacy`.

## 5. Permissions audit (release manifest)

The app declares INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS (reminders), RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (re-arm reminders after a reboot) and WAKE_LOCK (screen on during a mock). Plugins add VIBRATE (notifications), FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SHORT_SERVICE (WorkManager) and, until this change, USE_BIOMETRIC and USE_FINGERPRINT (androidx.biometric; the app never shows a biometric prompt).

Change on this branch: the two biometric permissions are removed from the merged manifest with `tools:node="remove"` in `AndroidManifest.xml`. No exact-alarm, location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone or advertising-id permission exists. Verified on 16 Oct with aapt2 on a release APK built from this branch (arm64, targetSdk 36, versionCode 1): 8 permissions, namely the 5 the app declares plus VIBRATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SHORT_SERVICE, and AndroidX's own-package signature permission `in.mockmob.app.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`, which is not requested from the user. Foreground-service declaration, if the Console asks: short background sync of queued answers; `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` is not declared.

Full description (4000 max; the 14 Oct text, 3,200 characters, still matches the code):

```
MockMob is a practice app for CUET UG 2027 students. A mock should show you where you lost marks and what to do about it, so the app works in three steps: practise, see where the marks went, repair the mistakes.

Practise
- Quick practice of 5 to 20 questions, or pick up to 12 chapters and practise only those.
- A full mock, timed like the exam, with a question palette, mark for review and a clear submit step.
- NTA mode: an exam-day screen with 50 questions in 60 minutes, modelled on the computer-based test, so the layout is familiar on the day.
- Marking follows the CUET pattern: 5 marks for a right answer, minus 1 for a wrong one, 0 for a blank.
- A question stream for untimed practice: answer, then see why.
- If your connection drops during a mock, your answers stay on your phone and are sent when you are back online.

See where the marks went
- Each result breaks your marks down by chapter.
- Your record shows marks session by session and ranks chapters by the marks you left open.
- Ask your record a question, such as which chapters cost you the most marks. Answers about your results come from your results. An optional AI mentor can also reply, and its replies can be wrong, so check them against the answer key and your textbook.

Repair
- Review groups your mistakes by chapter. Repair them one at a time and read the key explanation. Skipped questions are listed separately and are not counted as mistakes.
- Today suggests one next step from your own record and says why. Start it, or choose something else.
- Concepts has short lessons and recall cards from the chapters you practise.

Weekly rhythm
- Set a goal of 1 to 7 study days a week. A study day is a day you finish a practice, a mock or a repair. Pause the goal when life gets in the way; paused weeks do not break your run.
- Reminders are optional and are set on your phone. Choose which ones you want and your study time, and switch them off at any time.
- Mobi, the MockMob mascot, adds a short line on Today and after a session.

CUET tools
- Admission Compass shows which Delhi University programmes your subjects open under the UG Bulletin 2026-27, with college cutoffs by category and round from the 2026 CSAS lists.
- Combo planner works out which papers open the courses you want.
- The CUET 2027 guide has expected dates and a month-by-month plan.

Subjects
MockMob starts with the CUET UG Commerce papers: English, Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics.

Your account and your data
- Sign in with a code sent to your email, with Google, or with a password you set yourself.
- There are no ads in the app.
- Usage data is optional. It is off by default and can be turned on only by someone who says they are 18 or older. You can turn it off at any time.
- Delete your account from the app (You, Account, Delete account) or at www.mockmob.in/account-deletion.

Not affiliated
MockMob is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the National Testing Agency (NTA), the University of Delhi or any government body. CUET is an examination conducted by NTA. For official notices, dates and rules, use cuet.nta.nic.in.

Questions or feedback: support@mockmob.in
```
