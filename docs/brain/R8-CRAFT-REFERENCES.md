# R8 reference board: what each reference teaches, and what we must not copy

Rule for this pass: borrow principles, never appearances. Each reference earns its place by one
behaviour MockMob needs. If a change cannot name its reference principle (or the owner's own words,
or a general UX principle), it does not ship.

| Reference | Principle it demonstrates | Where MockMob applies it | Do NOT copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duolingo lesson check (wrong answer) | The verdict, the right answer and the one way forward arrive together, in the same place the student was looking; the next button is the only loud thing. | Result answer row and the demo's wrong-answer state: verdict + right answer + the chapter + one next action in one block. | Owl palette, cartoon celebration on every tap, streak pressure, hearts. Owner: "Duolingo level" motion is the floor, not the target; over-gamified is rejected. |
| Revolut transaction detail | Every number explains itself on tap: amount, category, where it came from, what you can do next. Numbers are tabular and never jump. | Score, marks lost per chapter, credits: each figure says its denominator and source; tapping a chapter shows its questions. Tabular numerals on scores, timers, prices. | Dark-blue fintech gradient cards, card-art carousel, ALL-CAPS labels. |
| Apple Fitness / Health summary | One headline number, then a short ranked list of what moved it; detail is one tap deeper (progressive disclosure, not deletion). | Result front stays (Keep); "Where your marks went" ranks chapters; detail opens in place. | Rings as decoration, gradient fills, the SF type system. |
| Apple Liquid Glass (iOS 26 bars and sheets) | Material belongs to chrome that floats over content (bars, sheets); content stays solid and legible; the material reacts physically. | App dock/sheets only, as already approved. Web: dock blur stays behind @supports. | Glass on cards or question text; tinted glass panels; blur on the exam screen. |
| HIGH (high.live) and Fampay | Confident motion with weight: elements arrive in order, one indicator slides, press feedback is immediate. | Segmented switches (Pro/Free, theme, tabs): one indicator slides; keys collapse flush on press within 1 frame. | Neon, glow, youth-slang copy, sticker overload. Owner: Night stays subtle. |
| Linear issue view (keyboard + state) | Every control has visible hover, focus, pressed and disabled states; keyboard users get the same path. | Focus rings on every Arena control (3px ink / volt on slate), Escape closes sheets and returns focus. | Purple-on-black, command-palette-first navigation. |
| Tinder / taste-picking first-run hint | A gesture is taught by showing it once, with visible buttons that do the same thing. | Key cards swipe rating: first-run nudge plus always-visible Missed / Got it / Easy buttons. | Card-swipe as the only input; dating-app copy. |
| Undertone / GameBlazers marketing sites (from the video) | The website reuses the product's own controls and the same numbers, so the promise and the product feel like one thing. | The hero demo, Mistake Lab story and the Arena use one illustrative attempt: same subject, question, answer change, chapter and next step. | Their palettes, layouts, prize art. |
| Railcode (from the video) | The metaphor belongs to the company. | Ours is the lamp-lit study desk, the answer sheet, Mobi and the missed question's route. Use them where they explain an action. | Pixel art, rollercoasters, any new metaphor. |
| Ideogram (from the video) | Identity can live in one concentrated decision. | Protect the lowercase wordmark with Mobi as the second o; check small-size legibility and alignment, nothing else. | Serif wordmark, organic brain mark. |

Sources of authority, in order: the owner's explicit words (ledger, taste profile) > his research
brain > the principles above. A reference never overrides a Keep item.
