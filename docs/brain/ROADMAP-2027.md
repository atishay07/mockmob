## Product and release

Score Recovery Lab: find mistaken reasoning, repair the missing step, test fresh
application, then check retention. Core actions use no paid model calls. Retain
Night Arena, existing practice modes and purchased tools. Do not sell a future
capability as live. The 2027 exam specification stays provisional pending NTA.

Eight initial pathways: sacrificing/gaining ratios and revaluation (Accountancy);
national income inclusions/exclusions and nominal/real values (Economics);
delegation/decentralisation and planning/controlling (Business Studies);
explicit statement/inference and main idea/supporting detail (English).

approved repair, independent solver or text evidence, and six distinct families.

publication. Required calibration: zero critical false accepts, category coverage,
at least 95% valid-reference survival, benchmark/authoring family separation.

## Engine contract

Wrong options create hypotheses. At most three probes; best hypothesis separation,
stable ID tie break. Two supporting observations from different families are
needed; contradictions produce a general explanation and another check, never a
definitive label. Confidence is optional and prohibited in strict NTA simulation.
Repair: distinction, worked contrast, student reasoning step, fresh application.
Responses: choice, numeric_step, evidence_span. No arbitrary free-text grading.
AI cannot choose truth, certify evidence or implement core scheduling/scoring.

Episodes: investigating → repairing → immediate_check → delayed_check_1 →
delayed_check_2 → maintained; also needs_repair, blocked_content, invalidated.
Reserve unseen IDs/families before starting. Assisted/repeated work cannot count.
First delayed check ≥24h after repair; second ≥72h after repair AND ≥24h after
first completed check. Both pass: “Passed two fresh checks.” Failure reopens
repair. Maintenance seven days later. Missing/changed/held content blocks or
invalidates claims, preserving history. Never claim mastery or recovered marks.

One server plan for Today, Radar, results and PrepOS: active session, overdue
check, unfinished repair, repeated supported miss, subject baseline, ordinary
practice fallback. Durations 10/20/30; one primary and ≤2 alternatives. Ordinary
mistakes remain reviewable independently of recovery qualification.

GET /api/learning/plan; POST /api/recovery/episodes; POST
/api/recovery/episodes/:id/responses. Existing /api/sessions owns scores/checks;
/api/recovery separates ordinary review and qualified progress. Durable owner-bound
sessions behind migration flag, legacy ticket completion preserved. Independent
scoring authority/content evidence/learning purpose. Creation, events, submission,
quota and payment retries idempotent. Deadline grace allows upload, not answering.
Invalid telemetry cannot discard valid ordinary scores. Never expose future keys,
diagnosis matrices or solutions before submission. Service-only tables, RLS,
owner indexes, uniqueness and append-only observations/versioned projections.

Decision replay follows the working repair loop: event mark effects and net
first-to-final separately, beneficial changes acknowledged, dwell estimated,
unanswered reasons unknown. One fresh strategy experiment, descriptive comparison.
Defer ranks, RL, probabilities, voice/social modes and live generation.
