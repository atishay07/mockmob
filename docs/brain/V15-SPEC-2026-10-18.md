## 2. Today (N1, top priority; the same design on the website, release 4, and in the app)

**What fails today:**
- the calendar/road strip shows many weeks, so only a few are in focus;
- the mock-count slider does nothing a student understands;
- the page asks the student to interpret it.

**The answer is three blocks, top to bottom, nothing else on Today:**

### 2.1 Up next (keep; he likes this direction)
- One move card: one volt key.
- A one-line reason that names the paper and date ("You lost 11 marks to changed answers in Mock 3, 16 Oct").
- "See why" and "Change length".
- A small "Something else" link.
- Done-for-today state: "Done for today. Tomorrow: {next move}".

### 2.2 Your road to CUET (replaces the strip AND the slider)

A single compact card:
- **Line 1, big and tabular:** "{N} weeks to CUET". Under it, small: "Expected window: May 2027" (or the student's date), with a quiet "Change" link.
- **Line 2, this week only, never a calendar:**
  - "This week: {d} of {D} study days" as D small dots (filled = done);
  - beside it, "{m} of {M} full mocks" as M dots.
  - Today's dot has a ring.
- **Line 3, the next milestone in words:** "Next: your first full mock by Sun 26 Oct" or "Next: Mock {k+1} by {day}". Computed from the plan; never more than one milestone.
- **One action:** "Adjust plan" (text link, not volt) opens a sheet.

### 2.3 Adjust plan sheet (replaces every slider)

- **Plain choices, not sliders:**
  - **Study days a week:** a segmented control 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 (one sliding indicator, N5).
  - **Full mocks before CUET:** three named plans as pressable rows, each with its computed total and pace:
    - "Light: one every two weeks (14 by May)"
    - "Steady: one a week (28 by May)", recommended, pre-selected
    - "Intense: two a week (56 by May)"

    Under the rows: "You have taken {n}."
  - **Exam date:** "Expected May 2027" (default) or "Pick my date".
- **"See all weeks":** a vertical list grouped by month. Each week reads "Week of 20 Oct: 2 of 4 days, 0 of 1 mock". The current week is highlighted, and past weeks are dimmed. This is the only place the whole road appears, so Today never shows a long strip.
- **Save:** "Saved to your account". The account fields study_goals.exam_date and mock_target are live on production; feature-detect the API.

### 2.4 Last paper and Your colleges (compact rows)
- **Last paper:** one row. Name, score with its denominator, the change against the previous paper, date and 12-hour time. Tap opens the result.
- **Your colleges:** one row. The top college and course, its status against DU's rules, and "Plan for it", which opens Compass.

**Moves elsewhere (nothing deleted):**
- the week ring and the weeks ribbon go to Progress (web) and You (app);
- milestones go to the Adjust plan sheet;
- the Mob card goes to /mob and Progress (TODAY-1017 move table).

**Mobi:** none on Today except a real moment (the first visit, or a goal met), at most once.

## 3. Result and Review (N12-N16; "not acceptable at all")

Restore the good stuff the 17 Oct redesign removed, and keep only the clarity additions.

### 3.1 Result, top to bottom
1. **The reveal moment:** the Mobi reaction, at most once (the reveal code exists from before 17 Oct; port it back), with the score and its denominator and a one-line "what happened".
2. **One next move with Repair's value:** one key, plus a line with the cost and balance.
3. **The full analysis, OPEN by default** (not folded behind a link), in this order:
   1. **Answer sheet:** kept; he likes it.
   2. **Where your marks went:** ONE unified weak-chapters list. Each row: chapter, marks lost of marks available, and a "Practise" key. This REPLACES "Chapters by marks left open", which he disliked.
   3. **Time:** per question against the median; the changed answers.
   4. **Strengths and weaknesses** (the SWOT content under plain headings).
   5. **Scores by session:** kept; "very intuitive".
4. **The mistakes list:** paper, date and 12-hour time on each; Repair and Check it held inline; a saved repair opens free.

  - the trap named in plain words;
  - the saved-repair state;
  - paper and time;
  - the "Try it again before you see the answer" step from web/fix-result-1017 (a good piece).
- One unified presentation; less clutter.
