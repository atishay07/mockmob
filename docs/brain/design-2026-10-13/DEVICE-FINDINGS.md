# Device findings, 13 October 2026 (orchestrator)

Real phone: the owner's Vivo V2413 (Android 16, 1260 x 2800 at 480 dpi, about 420 x 933 dp, 16 GB). Profile build of the real app for the signed-out onboarding, debug build of `test/device_demo/demo_main.dart` for the signed-in screens. Screenshots: `tmp/app-shots/vivo/*-s.png` (contact sheets `sheet0..6.jpg`). Earlier pass on the emulator at the Galaxy M06's 720 x 1600, 300 dpi: `tmp/app-shots/v2`.

What worked: cold start 0.73 s and warm start 0.45 s (profile); every onboarding step, hold to seal, save with the age question, "Not now", the first move, "Tonight at 8:00 PM", guest "Start now" asking for an account, the return screen ("Your sheet is where you left it"); in the demo build, Today, the dock, Start, Practice, the chapter confirm sheet, the runner, palette, submit, the practice result, the full mock, the sealed sheet drag unfold, all four recap cards, repair from Review to its ending, You, Appearance (glass stayed Full) and Daylight.

Fix these:

1. **Glass sheets let the screen behind show through.** In the chapter practice confirm sheet (`vivo/46-demo-after-start`), words from the Practice screen behind ("Choose your own", "Quick practice", "chapter") are readable through the sheet and through the inner "What you get" card. In the runner palette sheet (`vivo/55-demo-palette`) the question text shows through the top of the sheet. Text on a sheet must never compete with text behind it: make cards nested inside a sheet solid (raised token, no translucency), and raise the sheet's own tint so that, with or without the live blur, the backdrop is at most a faint colour wash (no legible glyphs). Check Full, Lite and Off.
2. **The save step leaves a large empty gap on tall phones** (`vivo/27-save`, `28-save-ready`, about 420 x 933 dp): the sheet card sits at the top and the age question and buttons sit at the bottom with a third of the screen empty between them. Distribute it: keep the age block and account buttons directly under the sheet card, or let the sheet card grow, so the layout reads as one group at every height from 360 x 640 to 430 x 950.
3. **Repair started from a result card says "We can't find this mistake any more."** (`vivo/64-demo-repair`). In the demo the result fixtures (Mock 7) and the review fixtures (Mock 2) disagree, so this may be fixture-only, but verify the real path: the route the result card opens (`/review/repair/:attemptId/:questionId?count=`) must find the mistake when the review data is not yet loaded or cached (load it, or fall back to the attempt's own wrong answers), and the not-found state should offer the next best repair rather than only "Back to Review". Make the demo fixtures consistent so the demo shows the real behaviour.
4. **Demo practice result shows placeholder fixture text** ("First", "Second", "Because 7"). Demo-only, but give the fixture real question and option text so device demos look true.
