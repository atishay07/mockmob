## 0. What "NTA mode" is on the website, in one paragraph

There is no separate NTA page. NTA Mode is `mode=nta` on the one runner at `/test` (`TestPageClient.jsx`). It is a Pro mode (`premium: true`, `creditCost: 0`): exactly 50 questions, exactly 60 minutes, one subject, original practice content, scored +5 / -1 / 0 on the server. When `mode=nta`, three things switch on that no other mode has: (a) the exam-integrity system (fullscreen, 3 warnings, copy/paste/right-click blocked, third warning submits), (b) the "Practice interface" toolbar offering two skins, "MockMob" and "NTA style", switchable mid-test with answers and timer intact, (c) the 50-question hard requirement. The "NTA style" skin (`data-interface='nta'`, the `nta-classic.css` file, entered with `&interface=nta`) is the conventional exam screen: blue header, white paper, shaped coloured palette chips, a green Save & Next and a purple Mark for Review & Next. The "exam-day layout" the owner means is this skin plus the integrity rules. The default skin ("MockMob") is the same layout in the website's dark Arena theme, with the plain buttons Previous and Next.

What the website does NOT have (so "every feature the website has" does not include them; section 9 lists them as app additions, clearly labelled): an instructions screen, candidate information, sections or section tabs, a question-paper view, a zoom or font-size control, a language toggle, a calculator, a rough-work pad, images or formulas in questions (text only), a separate "Save & Mark for Review" button, a 5 or 1 minute warning, a themed submit dialog (it uses the browser `confirm()`).

## 1. Launch and entry (website)

1. Practice page (`/dashboard`): a full-width card above the Questions step. Kicker "Closest to exam day", name "NTA Mode", badge ("Included" for Pro, "Needs access" locked, "Unavailable"), copy: "50 questions in 60 minutes on an exam-style screen: question palette, mark for review, clear response, a countdown that submits for you. Original practice questions, not official papers." Chips: "50 Q", "60 min", "Question palette", "Mark for review". A live miniature console on the right (blue bar #32688f with the subject and a ticking clock from 58:12, a 15-cell palette with a/v/m/n states, 7 px grey text lines on #eaf0f4, rotated 1.2 deg).
2. When selected: label "Exam screen", segmented control "MockMob style" / "NTA style", hint "Same questions, timing and scoring. You can switch inside the test." Non-Pro sees: "NTA Mode needs Pro. See how both screens look first:" with a link "Free exam-screen preview". Mobi companion copy on the setup page: title "Meet the exam before exam day." body "Choose your screen below."
3. Questions step is fixed: "Fixed for this mode". Launch URL: `/test?subject=<id>&count=50&mode=nta[&chapters=...][&interface=nta]` plus a `generationKey` (12 to 100 chars `[a-zA-Z0-9_-]`) added at launch; without it the runner errors "Missing generation key. Start a fresh test from the dashboard." Start button label: `Start NTA Mode`; while quoting "Checking…", unavailable "Not available".
4. Loading screen (`NtaValidationSpinner`): mono label "Preparing practice", heading "Building your verified {Subject} mock.", body "Selecting questions with current verification evidence before the timer starts.", three numbered steps "Checking available evidence", "Selecting your question mix", "Creating your session", footnote "This can take up to 30 seconds." (a volt dot pulses). Mobi companion above: "One question at a time." / "Opening your session. Your timer appears when the questions are ready; Mobi stays outside while you answer."
5. Server call: `POST /api/sessions` body `{subject, count:'50', mode:'nta', includeMeta:'1', generationKey, experience:'practice'}` (plus optional `sessionId` to resume). Response `{id, startedAt, expiresAt, serverNow, state, questions[{id,subject,chapter,question,body,options[{key,text}],passageText,passageGroupId,difficulty}], meta}`. The runner rejects anything but 50 questions: "The database returned {n} usable NTA questions for this subject; 50 are required." (or `meta.message`). Errors mapped: 402 premium/credits -> redirect `/pricing?reason=insufficient_credits`; `INSUFFICIENT_VERIFIED_CONTENT` -> "Verified questions are not ready for this selection yet. No credits were charged."; `INSUFFICIENT_PRACTICE_CONTENT` -> "There are not enough questions for this selection. Try fewer questions or another chapter. No credits were charged."; else the message or "Failed to load questions". Error view has a "Back to dashboard" button and a Retry.
6. Clock start: the deadline is from the server. `ends = receivedAt + (expiresAt - serverNow)`; `startedRef = receivedAt - (serverNow - startedAt)`. The timer is therefore already running when the integrity overlay asks the student to enter fullscreen ("Your session timer is already running.").

## 2. Integrity layer (NTA Mode only; `ExamIntegrityGuard.jsx`, `data/exam_integrity.mjs`)

- Policy: 3 strikes (`INTEGRITY_STRIKES = 3`). A violation is one of: `hidden` "You left the test tab", `blur` "The test window lost focus" (150 ms debounce), `fullscreen` "You exited fullscreen", `reload` "The session was restored" (a stored count is read back on reload). One violation per absence (`away` latch cleared when the student returns). Strike 3 ends the session and calls submit. Strike count is stored per session in localStorage and survives reload (a stored 3 submits immediately).
- Always-on while enabled: `copy`, `cut`, `paste`, `contextmenu` are `preventDefault`ed. Answering is locked (`canAnswer()` false) until the student has entered fullscreen and again after each violation until they tap resume.
- Dialog (modal `<dialog>`, cannot be cancelled with Escape), phases:
  - Entry: icon Maximize, title "Enter your exam space", body "NTA Mode uses the entire screen. Enter fullscreen to answer. Your session timer is already running." (if fullscreen unsupported: "This browser does not support fullscreen. Stay in this test window; leaving it still counts as a warning."), button "Enter fullscreen" ("Opening fullscreen…" while pending; "Continue in this window" if unsupported).
  - Warning: icon ShieldAlert, title "Warning {n} of 3", body "{reason}. The timer keeps running. Two more warnings will end and submit your session." (n=2: "One more warning will end and submit your session."), button "Return to fullscreen".
  - Terminated: title "Session ended", body "You reached three warnings. Answering is locked. We will submit the answers you already saved.", status "Submitting your saved answers…" / error text / "Confirming your result…", button "Retry submission" on failure.
  - Rules list (entry and warning): "Fullscreen exits, tab switches and leaving the window count as warnings." / "Copy, paste and right-click are disabled." / "The third warning submits your current answers."
  - Three 5 px bars show the strikes; fine print "Browser safeguards let you exit with Escape or switch apps. These exits are detected here; this is not server-verified proctoring."
  - Fullscreen error: "Fullscreen could not open. Allow fullscreen in your browser, then try again. The timer is still running."
- Pill under the header, right aligned: eye icon + "Warnings {n}/3" (1 = amber #fde68a, 2 or 3 = red #fca5a5), tooltip "Fullscreen exit or leaving the test is a warning. Warning 3 submits your answers."
- Dialog colours (dark, pinned in NTA skin): card #0f1612, line #536445, accent #d2f000, warn #fde68a, bad #fca5a5, button volt #d2f000 on #172000, radius 16.

## 4. Question panel

- Layout: a white paper card (`nta-paper`, max-width 930, right aligned in a grid `minmax(0,1fr) 292px`, gap 22) with: paper head, optional passage, the question block(s), the navigation row.
- Paper head (#f0f5f8, border #c4cfd6): kicker `MockMob CUET Test Console` (#455969 mono 10 px), `h1` = `Passage Set` when the current question belongs to a passage group, else `Question {n}` (22 px Arial #222). Right: two tag pills, the chapter name and the difficulty word (easy / medium / hard), mono 10 px caps.
- Passage block (only when a question has `passageGroupId` and `passageText`): a bordered box (NTA skin bg #eaf0f4 border #bdccd5), head row "Passage" left and the passage title right (`passageTitle` or `passageType` or default "Reading Comprehension"), body paragraph 15 px / 1.8, max 72ch. All questions of that passage group are rendered together, one after another under the passage, sorted by `orderIndex`; Previous and Next jump by group (block start - 1, block end + 1); opening a block marks every question in it Visited.
- Each question block: meta row, then `h2` question text (Arial #222, 21 px / 1.55 desktop, 18 px mobile; the NTA skin forces Arial), then options. Question text is plain text from `question.question` (no images, no LaTeX, no `body` shown on the web). 
  - Meta row left: "Question {n}" (the number is the 1-based position in the 50, accent colour) and a status chip whose label is the raw state string with `-` replaced by ` + ` (`answered`, `marked`, `marked + answered`, `visited`, `notvisited`; web quirk, do not copy those words; use the legend words). Right: text buttons "Mark" / "Unmark" (flag icon) and "Clear" (44 px min height). VoteControls are NOT shown in NTA Mode.
  - Active block (the current question) has a faint volt gradient left wash in the MockMob skin only.
- Options: vertical list, gap 10, each a full-width button (min 54, radius 8, 14 px text). Default skin: a 32 px rounded-square letter badge A, B, C, D (E allowed) and the text; selected = border rgba(210,240,0,.72), fill rgba(210,240,0,.08), badge fills volt. NTA skin: letters are hidden (font-size 0), a left radio circle 18 px (1 px #7f8b93 border, white); selected radio = fill #2769bd with a 4 px #eaf5ff ring and 1 px #2769bd outline; row background white (square corners, no border, no shadow); hover or selected row background #eaf5ff, border #c5dbeb. Options are `aria-pressed` buttons, disabled when pending submission, time up or submitting. Selecting the already selected option does nothing (use Clear). 
- Keyboard: Left/Right arrow = previous/next block; `1` to `4` select option A to D on the current question (blocked while the dialog or palette sheet is open, in inputs, or when `canAnswer()` is false).
- Every answer change records an event `{seq, qid, type:'answer', answer, at}`; every question open records `{type:'visit'}` (cap 5000 events, `at` = ms since server start).

## 7. Timer, time-up, submit and persistence

- Clock: 250 ms tick plus a `visibilitychange` re-read; `timeLeft = max(0, floor((endsAt - now)/1000))`; the deadline is the server's, never recomputed from the device's wall clock beyond the received offset.
- Time up: when `timeLeft <= 0` the runner auto-submits once (`autoSubmitTried`), no dialog, no countdown. After `endsAt` options are disabled and `chooseAnswer` refuses.
- Submit: `POST /api/attempts {sessionId, sessionTicket?, answers:{qid:int}, events (filtered at <= duration)}` (same handler as `PATCH /api/sessions`). Then: exit fullscreen, clear the draft and integrity keys from localStorage, set `sessionStorage mm:postTest = 1`, refresh the session, `router.push('/result/{id}')`. The button reads `Submitting...`. Accepted until `expires_at + 120000 ms` (`SUBMISSION_GRACE_MS`).
- Failure: banner (`role=alert`) "Submit failed: {message}. Your answers are saved. Press Submit again." with a "Retry submission" button. Offline: ticket session "Waiting for connection. Answers are held on this device. Reconnect before the two-minute submission window ends."; durable session "Waiting for connection. After the deadline, the server can score only answers it already received. Reconnect before time runs out." `pendingSubmission` is persisted; on the browser `online` event it resubmits automatically while inside the grace window. `SESSION_EXPIRED` -> "The server's submission window has ended. This session cannot be recorded."
- Event sync: durable sessions `PUT /api/sessions {sessionId, events}` every 1.5 s (cumulative); ticket sessions skip it.
- Persistence and resume: key `mm:test:{userId|anon}:{subject}:{chapter|*}:{count}:{difficulty|auto}:nta` in localStorage holds `{questions, answers, visited, marked, selectionMeta, generationKey, events, startedAt, endsAt, idx, pendingSubmission}` and is rewritten on every change. On reopening the same generation key or `sessionId`, `resumableDraft` accepts it when it has a `sessionId`, 1 to 50 questions, a finite `endsAt`, the same key, and `endsAt > now` (or a pending submission within the grace window). Reload, tab close and device sleep all resume at the saved question with the integrity strike count preserved. `beforeunload` shows "Your test is in progress. Leaving will save your spot but lose focus." Sessions are also resumable server-side by `sessionId` (durable mode); abandoned sessions are finalised at expiry from acknowledged events.
- Scoring (shown on Practice and the landing preview, not in the runner): +5 correct, -1 wrong, 0 blank; no correctness is shown during the session (no per-question check endpoint for `nta`).

### 9.1 NtaTypeSet: the exam skin's type (built 13 Oct 2026)

The exam look draws its text from `NtaTypeSet` (`flutter_app/lib/features/runner/nta/nta_skin.dart`), reached through `NtaSkin.of(context).type`. It is the web's forced Arial (section 4), with the platform sans behind it: Android has no Arial, so the phone draws Roboto, which is what Chrome on an Android phone shows for the website.

| Role | Exam look (NTA) | Size / line height / weight |
|---|---|---|
| Clock in the bar | Arial, tabular figures (the seconds never make it jitter) | 19 / 1.0 / bold |
| Subject in the bar | Arial | 18 / 1.15 / bold |
| Question | Arial | 18 x text step / 1.55 / regular |
