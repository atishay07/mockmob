## 1. Decisions (overrides of the proposal)

1. **Logo B keeps its small volt dot everywhere**: site bar, the opening's composed frame, footer. Draw it from src/components/Logo.jsx's own geometry (DOT_R / DOT_GAP). In the opening it is the last beat of the unfold: scale .6 to 1 plus opacity, 160 ms, starting at about 860 ms. A test asserts the dot is in the opening SVG and in the bar logo.
2. **Mobi's first frame has open eyes.** Hero and opening use the open-eyed wave pose (the rig's `wave` act or its settled pose), never the closed-eye `hello` squint. Closed eyes only for a blink.
3. **The two hero links stay**: "Free DU eligibility and cutoff calculator" (/cuet-cutoff-calculator) and "Try the exam-day screen" (#exam-experience), as ink underlined text links. Phone: directly under the sample card. Desktop: under the fact lines in the left column (fills the calm space there).
4. **The pointer spotlight is converted, not deleted**: it becomes Mobi's gaze. Desktop with a fine pointer only (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`): his eye group follows the pointer by at most 2.5 px on one compositor transform, updated in one rAF only while the pointer moves, and only while the hero is in view. Phones: no gaze, no per-touch work. Reduced motion: none. The grid, dot matrix and volt spotlight layers of HeroBackdrop go (slop C6/M7). Name this conversion in the commit message (L-005).
5. **Mobi glances at the headline when a typed phrase completes**: HeroTyped dispatches `window` event `mm:hero-typed` (detail: { index, final }); Mobi's eyes shift 2 px towards the h1 over 140 ms and back over 160 ms. On `final` he plays one small nod.
6. **The opening (loader)**, OpeningMo, timings as in the proposal, with this run gate:
   - `first` (first visit ever on this device): 1,600 ms; `short` (first load of a new browser session): 1,280 ms; `skip` otherwise.
   - Always `skip` for: reduced motion (device or the site's own data-motion switch), a hash or deep entry, a same-origin referrer (coming back to / from inside the site), a signed-in student (any cheap client signal the page already has, for example the existing auth cookie name the NavBar reads; if none is readable before paint, skip is decided after hydration and the layer is removed at once), and bots (navigator.webdriver or a bot user agent).
   - Any pointerdown, touchstart, wheel or keydown during the opening jumps straight to the hand-off (the FLIP and lift), so it never holds a student.
   - CSS-only animation on transform/opacity; ends `visibility:hidden` with fill-mode forwards even if JS fails; never runs twice in a session; the key is reachable (elementFromPoint) by 1.5 s at the latest.
   - L-043 is answered: logo B, about 1.3-1.6 s, skippable. One constant each: OPENING_FIRST_MS, OPENING_SHORT_MS.
7. **Typed line**: the proposal's one-pass select-and-type, phrases `HERO_TYPED = ['Fresh every time.', 'Every mark traced.']`, resting on the approved `"Know what to fix."` (HERO_LINES[2]). The DOM sentence stays "CUET 2027. One mock. Know what to fix." "Fresh every time" rests on the FAQ's approved claim (every mock is built fresh from one live bank). Caret ink, never volt, two blinks after the last letter then gone; never loops; pauses off-screen or tab hidden; reduced motion, signed-in and recovery variants: no typing.
8. **Night "CUET 2027." is a lamp-lit volt**, `--brand-word` Night = `#CDDD5A` (under 80% saturation; judged side by side with the key so both read as one family). Day `#526700`. `#D2F000` stays for fills only: the primary key, Mobi, the logo dot, a selected state with dark ink, the focus ring.
9. **Night raised surfaces by surface, not edges**: `--paper-raised` `#1B1A16`, `--paper-raised-2` `#211F1A` (at least 1.15:1 over `#12110E`), hairlines `rgba(244,240,230,.09/.16)`. Day cream tokens as in the proposal.
10. **One Mobi finish on the home page**: the rigged vector Mobi, live in the hero, generated static SVG poses in every seat (from the proven tmp/home-1104/design-brand/poses.mjs). The baked photo-real webp poses leave home-page seats only (they stay in public/ for OG images). Seat poses: think (Mistake Lab), read with the answer sheet (tour, exam section), encourage (Score Recovery), point (Compass, DU), cheer (close), attentive (pricing).
11. **Scroll (LK69)**: the proposal's controller, ported from the bench, with design C's guard: the non-passive touchmove is registered ONLY while a held story is within one screen of the viewport; elsewhere scrolling is 100% native and no listener can cancel it (a source test forbids non-passive listeners outside that window; this protects LK47's "no glide"). Statement: a 4-step held story on phones; desktop keeps its scrub plus a settle. No programmatic smooth glides: steps commit instantly, the stage content moves with a 180-240 ms CSS transform.
12. **Hero ambient pattern**: removed from the hero. The close band's static grid and dots stay but use neutral hairlines (no volt). Update data/tests/release9_public_1018.test.mjs accordingly (the "grid and dots stay" line was an agent's lock, not an owner ask; the aurora ban stays).
13. **Bubble**: fixed sizer (the widest of its lines), 180 ms opacity cross-fade with a 4 px rise, never changes size; lines come only from the sample's state (load / right / miss / after five), never from taps. Taps give one reaction act (the existing cycle) on both widths. Night's painted answer sheet fills one bubble per sample answer, not per tap.
14. **No 4-cell stat strip under the hero.** The second beat (lp-proof) becomes a hairline row of four sentence links (the facts unchanged), no big numbers, no glass.
15. **One volt key per viewport**: the bar's "Start free" is a raised-paper secondary while the hero key is visible.
16. **Typography**: the proposal's ladder. Literata 600 for every question stem on the home page; Literata preloaded on / only. Geist Mono leaves the home page. Numbers Bricolage 700 tabular.
17. **Exam screen**: the proposal's ExamScreen from the real runner (TestPageClient.jsx) anatomy, theme-aware, MockMob mode by default, an "NTA style" switch that keeps answers and timer, NTA vocabulary only in that mode. Heights: at most 930 px at 390, at most 760 px at 1440. Add examPreviewParity: every runner-facing string the MockMob mode shows exists in TestPageClient.jsx.
18. **Truth**: every typed phrase, bubble line and fact traces to a capability gate, OFFER, bankStats/QuestionBankLine (live count, never a hard-coded number) or DU_FACTS. A test lists each hero string with its source.
19. **Never cut**: every section, scroll story, showcase, hero link, animation, Mobi seat and the dock stay (L-004/L-005/L-042). Reveal entrances become a 180 ms opacity entrance (refined, not removed); the LiveStatsBand count-up stays; shelf object life stays but only the lit object floats.
