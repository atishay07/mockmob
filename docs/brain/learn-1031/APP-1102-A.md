## 0. Two rules that changed

1. **The middle dot is back, for a label and its length only.** R3 ruling W writes a time as `<label> · N min` ("Start chapter 1 · 10 min",
   "Continue · 6 min", "Revise 9 due cards · 3 min") and the owner's own LK27 words use it. APP-1101 section 7 banned middle dots; the copy
   lint of packet A now allows U+00B7 and still bans exclamation marks, arrows on keys, emoji, 24-hour times, "mastered", mastery %, "marks to recover".
2. **No zero is ever a lead (ruling Z, LK27).** A count that is 0 is dropped, never "0 due", "0 of 2", "Nothing in Revise yet".

## 1. Decisions for the owner

- **Navigation stays exactly as the app has it.** The web's "four equal tabs: Practice, Learn, Explore, Papers" (LK26) and "Saved moves into
  Explore's header" are web navigation. The app has no such tab row (the Practice doors are Practise | Your papers | Learn, plus the dock), so
  nothing is added: no tab, no door, no dock slot. If he wants the app's Practice doors to read as four equal tabs, that is a navigation change for him to decide.
- **Find opens on focus, as before.** The web's Find has its own address (`/learn/find`, the sidebar's "Key cards to revise" row). The app has
  no such row, so the search field is the way in: focusing it (or the link `find=1`) opens Find in place and, with nothing typed, draws the
  web's FindHome (the student's sets as rows, or a line and four words to try). Typing shows results; Cancel returns.
- **Saved is not ported** (it is a web Explore header key); the app's own saved questions stay where they are.
