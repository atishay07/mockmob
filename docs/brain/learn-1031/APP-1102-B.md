# APP-1102-B: the stage, the Explore feed, the chapter run and the end screens, brought to the live web (2 Nov 2026)

Packet B of the Learn v2 app update. Source of truth: the live web at origin/main, read-only worktree `wt-1102-web` (preview on 3452),
rounds 2 to 4 (R2-SPEC, R3-SPEC, R4-SPEC in `wt-1102-web/docs/brain/learn-1031/`), the owner's words of 1 Nov about 4:05 pm (LK24 to LK33)
and the code diff since the app's source commit 4bba8db6f. The app ports the phone band (under 768 px) only. Phone screenshots read:
`wt-1031-learn-v2/tmp/learn-1101/r4-merged/{f01-f09,r01-r06}-*-390-{night,day}.png`. Packet A does the data layer, home, chapter page,
Explore covers, Find and the Cards view; this packet owns `lib/features/learn2/{stage,feed,run}`, their tests and goldens, the parity
scripts `stage_text`, `feed_model`, `run_math`, and the blank rule in `keycards_lines.dart`.

option's `kind`, `chapterTitle`, `run.resume`, `run.fresh`, and `end?stop=1`), B reads it from the model's `raw` and asks A in the notes file.

### 2.9 Science text (R4 Science notation; `sciText`)
- A pure rule splits a card's words into plain, subscript and superscript runs: common ions from a table (SO42− becomes SO₄²⁻), formula tokens (H2SO4, (CH3)2NH), `[X]0`, `t1/2`, a complex ion's charge after its bracket, an electron count after an orbital (3d8), NCERT's en dash as a charge.
  Plain numbers, reaction names and vitamins are never touched. Display only; a screen reader reads the same characters. The stage draws it on a chemistry card's front, back, the question's line you needed, and the run's card, when the chapter key starts with `chemistry.`.
  Physics and Mathematics decks were published under the plain-text notation rule, so the web draws no notation for them and neither does the app.

## 5. Key cards' blank rule (kinds.js `blankRuns`, LK33)
- `blankRuns(front)`: the front split on its blanks, with the punctuation that touches a blank moved onto it as `lead` (an opening bracket or quote right before it) and `tail` (a comma, stop, colon, semicolon, question or exclamation mark, closing bracket or quote, ellipsis or percent sign right after it),
  so a line never starts with ", and" or ends with "(". No character is lost. Ported to `keycards_lines.dart` with a display parity case set (the web's own function over every blank front of the real decks, 965 cases, and the edge strings: 1004 in all).
  The stage's card face draws a gap and its punctuation as one unbreakable run. The Key cards' own `KcBlankText` (not in this packet's files) can adopt it with one call.

- Desktop and tablet bands: the session panel, the rail, keys line and the keys sheet, the "In Revise" tip beside the desktop pile, the "4 to revise" pile label (it only draws from 1024 px; the live region's "In Revise." is ported for a screen reader).
- The inert pages' 55% opacity (not visible at rest), `scrollToStemEnd` (an alias), the `held` page cache (the app keeps a controller per screen), `links.preview`.
- `html[data-motion='reduced']` mirrors: the app's `Motion.reduced` already covers both the system and the app's own setting.
- Science notation for non-chemistry subjects (the web draws none).
- The report menu on the run's card (still not in the app), the desktop pointer words of the chip ("Scroll or press ↓ for the next one").
- Watermarked print sheet, Find, covers, chapter page: other packets.

## 7. What the screen tests and the golden review caught
- The card page lost its scroll view when it became a flashcard, so a swipe that began on a card's front moved nothing (the pager's gate is decided by a scroll view's physics). The card page is again a scroll view exactly as tall as the page (`_CardScroll`); a pager test drags from a card's front and fails without it.
- Against the web's phone shots: the run's stage tag ("Due today") sits at the card's right edge (`tagRight`), an open mistake in the run wears the history mark, the run's Skip is a quiet underlined word (the feed's stays a line key), Free's day used starts at the top under the Close row (it was centred in the screen), and a text key is at least 48 dp wide ("Skip" was 41).
- A stop part way reads "N of M done" from the furthest item reached (the web's number), so a stop on the first card leads with the chapter, never "0 of 16".
- The recorded wrong answer's re-insert is a card from earlier ("Back from earlier"); the tag "The line you needed" is covered on a written card.
