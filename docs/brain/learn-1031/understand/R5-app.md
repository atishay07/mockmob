## 1. The big picture in six sentences

1. The app has FOUR separate "card" systems that all sit under the word Learn or Revise: Lessons (read a short lesson, `/api/study/units`), Concept cards (recall cards that come out of lessons, `/api/study/runs`, called "Revise N concept cards"), Key cards (NCERT lines, `/api/keycards*`), and Revise (the wrong-question queue, `/api/me/revise`, which also plays Key cards at its tail). They have different rating keys, different "Revise" meanings and different entry rows.
2. Learn is not a tab. It is the third segment ("Learn") of a switch under Practice's title (`Practise | Your papers | Learn`), URL `/practice?door=learn`. The dock is Today, Practice, Start (round centre key), Mob, You.
3. Key cards is one quiet text row inside that Learn door, below a "Read this next" lesson panel and a "Revise N concept cards" row. It has no tab, no Today row, no Start-sheet row.
4. Inside Key cards the structure is Home (due card, subject chips, "Part" picker, chapter list) then a chapter Deck page with a header block, an "Add chapter to Revise" button, a "Share chapter sheet" link, and a three-way switch Read / Study / Last look.
5. Study is one 165-card session ("1 of 165") with a fade-and-rise reveal and three rating keys. There is no flip, no rating animation beyond a cross-fade and rise, and no rounds (the website splits long chapters into rounds of 20).
6. "Must-know" is an `importance: must` flag drawn as an outlined pill on every Must card in Read, Study, Last look and Revise. In the demo chapter Accounting Ratios, 114 of 165 cards are Must-know (69 percent), which is why the tag reads as noise.

Page order (`_content`, lines 198-347):

1. Header block: unit label (only when it is not the title or book again), chapter title (`type.title`), NCERT line (`Accountancy: Company Accounts and Analysis of Financial Statements, Ch 5`), chip tag, and for Pro a "reason" block from `GET /api/me/chapters?subject=`: state line with ring (`Needs work`) and the reason (`7 of 12 right on the latest try at each question. 3. Action: `KeyButton.secondary` `Add chapter to Revise` (always grey, never lime). After add: `ReceiptLine` `Added to Revise. 12 cards.`; if every open Must-know card is already added: `In Revise`; offline: disabled with `Needs a connection.`; failure `The chapter was not added. Try again.` It adds the Must-know set only (Free: its first 5 taste cards). There is no per-card add in Read (the website has one).
4. Pro only: `KcLinkAction` `Share chapter sheet`. It builds plain text (title, NCERT line, then each Must-know card's front, back and reference) and opens the Android system share sheet (`_share`, lines 128-138; `keyCardsShareProvider` wraps `SharePlus.instance.share`). No print, no PDF, no watermark, no MockMob mark in the text. LK6 asks for a watermarked sheet. 5. `MmSegmented<KcMode>` with `Read`, `Study`, `Last look` (semantics label `How to go through the cards`). No sentence says what each does or how they differ (LK7); only the sub-lines inside each mode do.
6. The mode body. All modes share ONE `ListView.builder` (scroll is kept when the mode changes); Read builds its rows lazily because a chapter can hold 165 cards; a session fades in via `_modeFade` (`Motion.ui` 200 ms, `Motion.out`). Choosing a mode again starts a fresh session (`_session += 1`).

Net effect on a phone: header, counts, two actions and the switch use about 1080 px of a 1600 px screen before the first card (`13-deck-read-night-pro.png`: the card starts at y 1130 and its reference runs under the gesture bar).

### Read
`KcReadCard` (`lib/features/keycards/widgets/key_card_parts.dart:328-360`): kind label, `Must-know` pill, a `...` menu (Report); a small prompt line (omitted for term and key-line cards whose own line carries the blanks); the answer as NCERT prints it (`KcBack`, 17/26 reading size; lists with bullets or the book's own numbers; formulas on two tight lines; journal entries set out with `Dr.` at the right and credits indented; narration smaller); then the book reference (`Accountancy: ..., Ch 5, p. 194`), optional `Asked 3 times in CUET`, optional `In short. ...` note. Cards are separated by hairlines only. A chapter is one flat scroll with no grouping by NCERT section (though `source.section` such as `5.1 Meaning of Accounting Ratios` is on every card), no search, no jump, no filter by kind or Must-know (LK4). Free: the first five Must-know "taste" cards are whole; the rest collapse to one `KcLockedRow` (`160 more cards in this chapter`, `Pro opens every card in every chapter and keeps them on a schedule.`, `Upgrade to Pro`) placed where the first locked card stands (`_readRows`, lines 352-367; `31-deck-locked-day-free.png`).

### Study (the mode the owner likes)
`KeyCardRun` (`lib/features/keycards/widgets/key_card_run.dart:22-173`), mode `study`. `KcSessionKeys` adds hardware keys (Space or Enter shows the answer; 1, 2, 3 rate; `key_card_parts.dart` about 751-).

- Cards: every open card of the chapter in book order, Must-know and good-to-know mixed (`deck.cards`). Counter `1 of 165`. No rounds. (Website: `STUDY_ROUND = 20`, `Cards 21 to 40 of 165`, `Study the next 20`, `Study again from the start`, `Read the chapter`; `ChapterDeck.jsx:118`.)
- Front (`KcStudyFace`, `key_card_parts.dart` about 587-): kind line `1 of 165  Definition  [Must-know]  ...`, a chapter-name line only in Revise (`showChapter`), then a `MmSurface.plain` card with the question in `type.question`; the whole card is a tap target (press scale 0.99, selection haptic) plus the lime key `Show the answer`. Blanks of term and key-line cards are underlined gaps.
- Revealed: the same card grows; under a hairline the answer appears via `MmEntrance(rise: 8)` (fade plus an 8 dp rise), the question dims to `ink2`, reference and note show, and the lime key cross-fades (`MmOverlapSwitcher.fade`, `Motion.ui` 200 ms) to `How did it go` plus three equal grey keys `Missed` `Got it` `Easy` (`KcRatingKeys`, about lines 535-585; none is lime so no rating is nudged).
- Rating: `rateKeyCard` -> outbox (`OutboxKeyCardReviews`) -> `POST /api/keycards/review` `{cardId, rating, mode, clientReviewedAt}`; ratings missed=1, gotIt=3, easy=4 (`keycards_rules.dart:93-96`). The tapped key shows loading ink; on success (`sent`, or `waiting` when offline) `hapticsProvider.tick()` for Missed and `confirm()` (Android CONFIRM verb) for Got it and Easy; counts advance; the next card comes in by `MmOverlapSwitcher.fade(Motion.card 240 ms)` plus `MmEntrance(rise: 8)`; a TalkBack announcement says `2 of 165`. There is NO flip, no rating-specific animation, no card fly-off, no streak or progress bar, no sound.
- `KcKeepInView` (about line 697): after each reveal or next card the page auto-scrolls just far enough to show the keys (animated `Motion.ui`). In the walk this scrolls the header, Add and Share out of sight, and the rating row ends on the gesture bar (`14b-study-revealed-night-pro.png`).
- End (`KcSessionEnd`, lines 176-224): title `Chapter session done`, counts (`3 got it, 1 easy, 2 missed`), optional `Saved on this phone. It is sent when you are back online.`, lime key `Back to the chapter`, quiet link `Go through again`. Plain text, no motion.
- Free cap (`KcReviewCapNote`): `You have used today's 10 card reviews on Free`, `The rest wait until tomorrow. ...`, `Upgrade to Pro`. Failure line: `That card was not saved. Try again.`

### Last look
`KcLastLook` (`key_card_run.dart:228-336`): only Must-know cards (114 in the demo chapter), answers already showing, one at a time in `MmSurface.plain`, lime `Next card` key at right (last card: `Finish`), `1 of 114` at left, horizontal swipe (velocity over 200) moves on or back with a 240 ms slide (8 percent of width) plus fade; the line above reads `Must-know cards only. Nothing is scheduled.` End: `That was every Must-know card in this chapter.` with a secondary key `Go through again`. ## 6. Revise and "The line you needed"

Revise (`lib/features/review/revise/revise_screen.dart`, 1761 lines; copy `revise_copy.dart`): one run, `GET /api/me/revise?cap=&queue=1`, `POST /api/me/revise/answer`. Plays, in order: questions the student got wrong (MCQ, boxes 1/3/7/21 days), `Check it held` items, then Key cards due (up to 20, from `keyCardsDueProvider`, `GET /api/keycards/due?limit=20`) with the same `KcStudyFace` and `KcRatingKeys` as Study (`_rateKeyCard`, about lines 118-150; stage about line 436). Head `19 due today, about 5 minutes.` then a mix line (`3 questions back, 2 Check it held, 12 concept cards, 19 Key cards`); `20 of 30 due today` when the run holds part. When questions cannot be read the Key cards play alone with `Your questions could not be read just now. The Key cards can.` (`15-revise-head-night-pro.png`) or offline `You are offline. Your questions come back here when you are online.` End `19 Key cards reviewed`. Foot `Free. Not counted in your marks or accuracy.` The due queue is held while Revise is up so a rating never reorders it; offline it uses the kept queue ordered by the website's own rules (`KeyCardRules.dueQueue`, `keycards_rules.dart` about 452-484: due first, Must-know first, then new cards chapter by chapter up to the day's allowance; new cards pause when more than 40 are overdue).

Limits that bound a redesign (`keycards_rules.dart:5-30`, held equal to the website by test): retention 0.9; exam horizon `2027-05-15`; Free 5 taste cards a chapter (Must-know only), 10 reviews a day, 5 new cards a day, 3 "line you needed" a day; Pro new cards a day 10, 15 or 25 (default 15); 15 seconds a card for minutes.

The line you needed (`lib/features/keycards/widgets/line_you_needed.dart:19-168`): under a wrong answer on result screens. `GET /api/keycards/line?questionId=` returns `{cards (max 2), source: linked|matched|none, left}`. Block (`MmSurface.inset`): header `The line you needed` and caption `Linked to this question` or `Matched by its words`; per card the kind caption, compact answer (`KcBack(compact: true)`, 15.5 pt) and reference; then `KcLinkAction` `Add to Revise` (adds both via `POST /api/keycards/add {cardIds}`, then `In Revise` with a check) and for Free `2 more today on Free`. Free after 3 reads: the block becomes a Pro gate (`KcLineGate`), shown once per screen. A read is kept alive per question so reopening does not spend another. Hidden when Key cards are off, the read fails, or no line is found. `16-line-practice-result-night-pro.png`: the block sits between Explanation and the Repair copy, with no motion and no link to the chapter's deck.

## 7. How the app fetches cards (endpoints)

All in `lib/features/keycards/data/keycards_api.dart` (`KeyCardsPaths`, lines 60-70), implemented by `HttpKeyCardsApi`:

| Call | Used by |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/keycards` | home (`keyCardsHomeProvider`, autoDispose, no hidden retry; a 404 `KEY_CARDS_OFF` hides every entry; body saved to the kept copy) |
| `GET /api/keycards/deck?chapter=<key>` | deck (`keyCardsDeckProvider.family`; raw body kept with a sha under `keycards_deck_<chapterKey>`; opened decks are the offline set) |
| `POST /api/keycards/add` `{chapter}` or `{cardIds}` | Add chapter to Revise, The line you needed |
| `GET /api/keycards/due?limit=20` | Revise's Key cards tail |
| `POST /api/keycards/review` | every rating (through the outbox, idempotent key `reviewKey(cardId, at)`) |
| `GET /api/keycards/line?questionId=` | The line you needed |
| `POST /api/keycards/report` `{cardId, reason<=300}` | the `...` menu; four reasons: `The text does not match the NCERT book`, `The answer is wrong or incomplete`, `The question is unclear`, `Something else` |
| `GET /api/me/chapters?subject=` | Pro's state reason on the deck header |
| `PUT /api/me/subject-options` | the Part picker |

Other Learn calls: `GET /api/study/catalog`, `POST /api/study/runs`, `GET /api/study/runs/:id`, `POST /api/study/runs/:id/events`, `GET /api/study/summary`, `GET /api/study/units/:id` (`lib/features/learn/data/study_api.dart`); `GET /api/learning/plan` (`lib/core/repositories/plan_repository.dart:36`); `GET` and `PUT /api/study/preferences`. Free gates: the server answers 402 `PRO_REQUIRED` (`KeyCardsProRequired`, reason `keycard_reviews` or `keycard_line`). Wiring: `lib/app_overrides.dart:31-40` (`liveAppOverrides({keyCards: true})`); the default provider is `OffKeyCardsApi`, so any screen or test without the override shows nothing.

Card JSON (`KeyCard.tryParse`, `keycards_models.dart:54-135`): `id, v, subject, chapterKey, chapter, unit, kind, front, back, quote, blanks[], source{kind, book, code, chapterNo, page, section}, importance 'must'|'good', asked (past CUET questions that test it), tags[], order (book order), note ("In short")`. Free's locked stubs: `{id, kind, label, locked: true}`. Kind labels: Definition, Term, Key line, List, Difference, Steps, Types, Formula, Fact, Example, Entry, Quote, Word, Idiom, One word, Usage, Rule (`keycards_copy.dart:134-153`). Nothing in the payload carries a topic grouping other than `source.section` and `tags`. Chapter home row: `key, title, ncert{book, chapter}, chip, cards, mustKnow, unlocked, added, due, state{state, label, fading}`.

Haptics and motion inventory for Key cards: pressables use `MmPressHaptic.selection` (chapter row, link actions, reveal tap, mode switch); rating success tick or confirm (`lib/core/haptics/haptics.dart`; the verbs gestureStart, gestureEnd, segmentTick, toggleOn and toggleOff exist and Key cards do not use them); `Motion.ui` 200 ms, `Motion.card` 240 ms, `Motion.press` 100 ms, enter 360 ms (`lib/core/motion/motion_tokens.g.dart`); reduced motion honoured in `KcLastLook` and `KcKeepInView`. Available in the app but unused for Key cards: `lib/core/motion/flight.dart`, `drag_commit.dart`, `object_react.dart`, `springs.dart`, `scrub.dart`, `lib/core/widgets/count_up.dart`, and Learn's `widgets/card_stack.dart` (a paper-card pile mark that fans out, breathes three times and slides off) and `widgets/run_pips.dart`.

## 9. Problems seen (concrete, with where)

1. Key cards is the third quiet row of the Learn door, below a full-height lesson panel and a concept-cards row; the Practice badge `9+` and the dock overlap the list in `01-learn-door-night-pro.png` (LK1). The same title weight is used for three different kinds of cards.
2. Four card systems, four meanings of "Revise": the wrong-question queue, the concept-cards run (`Revise 12 concept cards`), Key cards' `Revise now`, and `Add chapter to Revise`. `Revise now` on Key cards home opens a run that may start with questions.
3. The deck page spends about two thirds of the first screen on header, counts, Add, Share and the switch before a card (`13`, `14`). In Study the card is at y 1180 and the keys at 1390; after reveal the page scrolls and the rating keys end on the gesture bar (`14b`).
4. Read has no structure: one flat list of up to 165 cards, hairlines only, no grouping by NCERT section, no search, no jump, no filter (LK4).
5. Must-know is a pill on 69 percent of cards in the demo chapter, in every mode including Study fronts and Revise (LK13). Rule: `importance == 'must'` only (`KcKindLine`, `key_card_parts.dart:90`); the deck header repeats it (`114 Must-know`).
6. Read versus Last look is a filter plus pagination of the same card face, with no explaining sentence (LK7).
7. Study has no rounds, so a 165-card chapter is one long countdown; choosing a mode again resets the session; nothing is lost server-side because each rating is sent.
8. No motion of consequence on Got it: success is a haptic plus a 240 ms fade and 8 dp rise (LK5). Learn's concept run and `card_stack.dart` show the app can do more.
9. Share chapter sheet is raw text with no watermark or MockMob mark, so it is copyable (LK6). Pro only.
10. The Part picker pushes chapters off the first screen and re-lays the whole list after a save with no transition (LK2).
11. Learn's chapter list and Key cards' chapter list are unrelated lists; no `Key cards` link inside `ChapterFold` though `KcHome.chapterFor` can map them; Learn's chips, Key cards' chips and Practice's subject are three separate states.
12. Steering: the only suggestion is one lesson chosen by costliest open-mistake chapter (`conceptsPick`) plus `Where your marks slip` (LK9, LK11). No time choice, no mix of cards and questions, no "what is where" map (LK10).
13. Today and the Start sheet offer neither Learn nor Key cards.
14. Copy drift against the website: straight versus curly apostrophe, `?mode=last` versus `?mode=last-look`, several sentences missing (section 8).
