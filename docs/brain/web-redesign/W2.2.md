# W2.2 Public and SEO pages (web)

14 October 2026. Package W2.2 of `WEB-REDESIGN-PLAN-2026-10-14.md` (sections 7.8 and 11, Phase 2). Branch `web/W2.2`, cut from `feat/web-redesign-p2` (W2.1 merged). Owner defaults accepted: D5, D6 (glass on chrome only, light as environment only), D7 (screenshots and owner OK before a visible UI is replaced), D8, D10, logo B final. Nothing here is deployed.

## Job

Put every public page on the Night Sheet tokens and the new header and dock, make the calls to action follow the visitor (a signed-in student sees their own action, not Start free), and give the three legal pages one shared layout.

## Outcome

1. One editorial system for the public pages: `src/components/public-pages.css` (classes `pp-*`) with three server components in `PublicPage.jsx` (page head, "on this page" list, closing panel) and one client piece in `PublicCta.jsx` (the member action).
2. Member-aware calls to action. `PageClose` renders the guest copy, the member copy and every action variant; `<html data-visitor>` picks one before first paint (`vs-guest-only`, `vs-member-only`, `vs-new-only`, `vs-returning-only`, `vs-hide-pro` from `src/app/visitor.css`). A guest sees Start free (a returning visitor sees Log in first), a signed-in student sees Resume your test, Start your 10-question check or Continue practice, the same one action the header and the dock show (`memberDockActions` in `src/lib/navigation.js`). The server HTML stays the guest page, so crawlers and the SEO text are unchanged.
3. `SeoContentPage` (the six CUET guides, the three SEO landing pages and four subject hubs): head with breadcrumb, a contents list beside the text on desktop (sticky), a 66 character measure, link pills with no arrows, the FAQ in the kit's text and focus, and the closing panel at the end. JSON-LD (breadcrumb, FAQ, course) is unchanged.
4. Legal pages: `LegalPage.jsx` holds the layout (title, date, contents, sections, links to the other two policies, plain print). `privacy`, `terms`, `refunds` keep every word of their wording; only the markup changed (the merged sections in the privacy page are separate sections again).
5. About, contact, features, pricing and the PrepOS credits page are rebuilt on the same parts. Features loses the dot patterns, the glass cards, the numbered badges, the orb, the hidden fake-numbers block and "Join the Mob"; contact lanes name their action ("Email support") instead of repeating the address four times; pricing keeps its plan data, offer logic and payment buttons.
6. DU tool pages (cutoff calculator, subject combination planner): the aurora, grid and dots backdrop, the "New · Combo Planner" kicker, the numbered step badges and the gradient wash on the best combination are gone; the second line of the heading is quieter instead of volt; a member-aware closing panel and the mobile dock are added to the calculator. The tools themselves (`components/du`) are untouched.

## Data truth

No data changes. The pricing offer still comes from `publicOffer()`, plan ids and amounts from the same fields, and the comparison rows from `COMPARISON_GROUPS` or `RECOVERY_PACKAGING_ROWS`. The two truth notes that sat after the footer on /pricing now sit under the comparison table. An unknown PrepOS balance shows "Not available", never a number. The features page's Compass sample stays labelled "Illustrative example, not a prediction"; its hidden block of invented 184/200 style figures is removed.

## States

Visitor: new, returning, member (free, Pro, no scored session yet), member with a saved test (Resume your test), and not reachable (the stored hint decides, as everywhere). Page: pricing for a Pro member hides "See what Pro includes" and the free card shows their own action. Layout: 360, 375, 1024, 1366, 1920; Night and Daylight; reduced motion (the page-head settle-in and the pill press stop); print (policies and DU pages drop the header, footer and panels).

## Persistence

None. No new storage keys.

## Feel

Calm, editorial, quiet. Large Bricolage headings with the condensed cut for numbers, hairlines and space before boxes, one lit surface (the closing panel, with the volt top edge) per page. Motion: the page head settles in once (about 320 ms, opacity and a 10 px rise) on the pages people land on, never on articles or policies.

## Conflicts settled

- The 14 Oct plan says "no change to the SEO substance". Body copy, headings and FAQ answers are untouched; changes are limited to sentence-case for the h1 on `/cuet-2027`, breadcrumb case, and removing the unused `eyebrow` strings.
- The contact dock note said "Support replies within 24h." Nothing in the product backs a reply time, so it now says "Questions? Email us."
- SEO titles keep their casing (they are search copy); only the em dashes in the About and Contact titles became colons.
- `html, body { overflow-x: hidden }` (globals.css) makes `<body>` a scroll container, which stops `position: sticky` working (the header and the contents list). `public-pages.css` applies `overflow-x: clip` to pages that contain `.pp-hero`, the same fix `landing.css` uses for the home page. Pages built on the old classes outside this package still have the old behaviour.

## How it was built (for the packages that follow)

- New page: `<PublicHero>` for the head, `pp-section` and `pp-section--sunken` for bands, `pp-split` for a heading beside its content, `pp-defs` for definitions, `pp-lanes` for rows with an action, `<PageClose>` last. Use `buttonClass()` from the kit for links that look like buttons. No per-page `<style>` blocks.
- The guest side of a member-aware control is a link with `vs-guest-only` (or `vs-new-only`), the member side is `<MemberAction />`.
- Tokens only: every colour, size and space is a `--mm-*` role, checked by `data/tests/public_pages.test.mjs` (no undefined token, no caps costume, no gradient, no backdrop blur, no em dash, no trailing arrow).
- Page styles that need to go beyond the shared file live beside the page: `pricing/pricing.css`, `cuet-cutoff-calculator/du-page.css` (loaded by both DU pages), `cuet-subject-combination/combo-planner.css`.
