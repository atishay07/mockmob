## Experience brief

### Login and signup

- **Job.** A visitor wants in. A returning visitor (S2) wants to log in, not to be sold to; a stranger (S1) wants to know it is free and quick. They came from the header, a gated page (`next`), or a pricing link.
- **Outcome.** One primary action on the screen: the email code (volt). Google sits beside it as the secondary. After sign-in the saved `next` page opens, else onboarding for a new account, else Practice (`postSignInPath`, unchanged).
- **Data truth.** No numbers on these pages. The promise is the owner-approved line, split into its three steps. Nothing claims a count or a result.
- **States.** Checking the session (members only, read from the visitor hint before paint, so a stranger never sees a spinner); the form; sending; code sent; checking the code; error (from `SignupCard`, unchanged); rate limited; in-app browser notice; MockMob unreachable (the form stays, with a line saying sign-in needs a connection); signed in (a quiet "Opening MockMob" status while the redirect runs). On `/signup`, a visitor who has signed in on this device before sees "Log in" first (`vs-returning-only`).
- **Persistence.** Nothing new is stored. The `next` path is saved by `SignupCard` exactly as before.
- **Feel.** Reassure. A warm Night sheet with lamp light on the left (pinned Night even when the page is Light, the way an app's hero is), the form on paper or charcoal on the right. One authored moment: the three promise lines arrive in sequence; reduced motion shows them at once.
- **Acceptance.** Below.

### Onboarding (first run and `?edit=true`)

- **Job.** A new member tells MockMob which papers they are writing, so Practice and Today have something to plan from. An existing member arrives from "Edit subjects" on Practice.
- **Outcome.** Choose up to five subjects, save, then the first check: "Start with 10 questions in {subject}" opens Practice with that set prefilled (`/dashboard?subject=&mode=quick&count=10`), where the confirm line shows the cost. The app's two-minute sheet deck is not ported (plan 7.7).
- **Data truth.** The list is `/api/subjects`; only `practice === 'supported'` can be picked. Saved subjects practice cannot open stay visible with a Remove action, never offered as new picks. "Saved" appears only after the server answered and the session refreshed.
- **States.** Loading (a skeleton the shape of the tiles), catalog error (retry), account unreachable (retry, nothing lost), signed out (to signup, as before), choosing, at the cap (a written line, not a silent no), saving (button keeps its label width), save failed (choices kept), saved (first run: the first-check step; edit: back to Practice as before).
- **Feel.** Welcome. Mobi greets once, in a small pool of lamp light. Each subject tile carries its glyph in the app's subject light field (`light_fields.dart`), so the grid reads as a set of real papers, not identical boxes. The chosen state is the answer-sheet bubble: hollow at rest, inked when chosen, plus a raised fill and an ink rim (never colour alone).

### Account (`/profile`)

- **Job.** Check who is signed in and what plan, change the name, subjects, theme, glass and start page, manage privacy and sign-in.
- **Outcome.** No dominant action until something is edited; then that section's Save is the one primary. Free members see "See Pro" in the plan section (PlanCard, unchanged logic).
- **Data truth.** Everything comes from the confirmed session (`useAuth().user`) and is painted at once: the page no longer waits on `/api/attempts` (the old "Mocks taken" and "Avg score" tiles, which mixed quick and full sessions into one percentage, are dropped; Progress and Review hold the record). Plan and credits are the server's (`isPremium`, `premiumUntil`, `creditBalance`). Theme, glass and start page are per-device and say so.
- **States.** Session loading (skeleton sections), name: rest, editing, invalid (empty, over 80), saving, saved at a 12-hour time, offline, failed; subjects: catalog loading, catalog failed (names fall back to readable ids, editing waits), editing, at cap, keep at least one, saving, saved, failed; appearance: storage blocked (applies for this visit, says so), reduced transparency (glass is solid whatever is chosen, says so); start page, usage data, sign out of other devices and delete account keep their existing states.
- **Feel.** Clarify. A settings page in the editorial pattern: each group has a heading and one line on the left, its controls on one raised surface on the right (stacked on phones). No nested cards, no mono labels, no kickers.
