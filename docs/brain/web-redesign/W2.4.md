## Job

A signed-in student opens one of the record pages to answer one question: what did I miss and how do I repair it (Review), what has my practice recorded (Progress), where are marks being lost (Weak chapters), which DU courses are open to my subjects (DU Compass), what does my record say (Ask), what did I keep (Saved), one more question (Explore), a lesson (Learn), where do I stand on practice points (Leaderboard).

## Outcome

- Every page opens with the kit `PageHead`: the plain name as the title (D10, the same labels as `src/lib/navigation.js`), one lead line, the brand name only as a descriptor in the lead ("Radar reads your saved sessions...", "PrepOS answers from your own saved practice..."). No eyebrows, kickers, numbered badges, middle-dot meta strings or trailing arrows. Page titles in the tab follow ("Weak chapters | MockMob").
- Review leads with one lit object: the record thread Found, Repaired, Checked, Holds (D9), as four bubbles on a thread (the app's life row on the web), with one dominant action: "Open your latest misses" (the newest session with misses) or "Start practice" when nothing is waiting. Below: the sessions (newest first, "8 Oct. 12 of 15 right.", "3 to look at"), the repair record (found and repaired dates, the check state in words), then the recall cards.
- Progress: a ruled facts strip with denominators ("142 of 230" answered right; no percentage under 20 questions), the next step, milestones, the study record, fresh checks and the exam playbook, without build words ("episode", "calibration", "certify").
- Weak chapters: the record view unchanged, the Chart.js line replaced by plain token-themed bars (one series, the accent, latest labelled, a screen-reader list of every value), subjects as aligned columns, and a written note while the sample is thin.
- DU Compass: page head, the three checks as plain lines (no "1. 2. 3."), one note that prevents a wrong impression (published history, not a prediction), the practice bridge as a solid surface (no volt-wash gradient), sources without middle dots.
- Ask: page head with the cost stated (record answers free, an optional written reply 1 PrepOS credit). The assistant's own older head is hidden by `mentor/ask.css` until `MockMobAIHub.jsx` drops it.
- Saved: answering or unsaving a card no longer reloads the whole list (it used to drop every revealed answer and flash the skeleton); an unsave can be undone on the card and the count follows.
- Explore: own page client (same feed logic as `DiscoveryFeed.jsx`), kit fields, kit states, the side column without the "Tonight's rule" kicker or arrow glyphs; `explore.css` moved to the tokens with the answer-sheet option language (key bubble, wrong surface, correct in words and icon), no 3px side border.
- Leaderboard: plain table with the student's own place first ("You are 14th of 120 on this board"), "Practice rival" on every simulated row, no "01" place badges, no "Mobber".
- Learn: a labelled loading state instead of "Loading Learn...", tab titles without em dashes.

## Data truth

No API or data changes. Found counts what the server counts (`ordinaryReview[].mistakes`: missed or left blank) and is labelled that way. Repaired comes only from stored repairs (`/api/me/repair-record`, up to 100, shown as "100+" when full). Checked counts `checkedState: passed`; while the server reports `not_available` (recovery gate closed) Checked stays a dashed, written "not open yet". Holds has no source yet and always says so. Unknown is never drawn as 0; a failed repair record says it could not be read and offers a retry without hiding the sessions.

## States

Each page: loading (the page's own shape), offline (OfflineState, retry), error (ErrorState, work kept, retry), empty (EmptyState with Mobi and one action), thin evidence (Weak chapters: 1 or 2 sessions), ready. Review also: repair record loading, failed, empty; nothing waiting (all clear). Leaderboard: not on the board yet.

## Feel

Calm desk at night. One authored moment: on Review, the Found to Repaired run of the thread draws in volt over 420 ms (emphasized curve) when a repair exists; reduced motion shows it finished. Everything else is still: press about 100 ms, state changes 200 ms, transform and opacity only.
