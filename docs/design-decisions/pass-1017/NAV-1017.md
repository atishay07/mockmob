## 0. The decision in six lines

1. **App bar: five destinations in one glass capsule, plus a separate round Start key at its trailing end.** Today, Practice, Review, Colleges, You in the capsule; Start is a 64 dp circle of the same height beside it, 8 dp away. Both sit in one 64 dp row, so every icon, every label and the key share the same two measured centre lines and the key's top and bottom edges are the capsule's edges. (Option A.)
2. **Start stays an action, not a tab, and is not in the middle.** It moves from slot 3 of 6 (31 dp left of the screen centre, a lime pill filling 81% of the bar) to the thumb-side end of the row. The lens never has to cross it.
3. **Nothing is cut.** Colleges stays a named tab (it answers F2 and L22: Compass and My colleges are one tap from the bar). You stays a tab. Learn on the website moves from the dock into the More sheet and the Practice page; that is a move.
4. **The key does not follow page state.** Its look is fixed: glass, with a lime play glyph and a lime ring. It never turns white, outlined or solid. That is also what keeps one lime fill per viewport. A lime-filled version (A1) is captured so the owner can overrule this on the emulator.
5. **The website phone dock gets the same structure at 390:** a floating glass capsule with Today, Practice, Review, Colleges, More, and the same Start key beside it, not a disc rising out of the bar.
6. **Liquid Glass taste is carried at app level**, not Android's: a lens that lifts and follows the finger, springs with a little bounce only after a flick, scroll-minimise, saturated blur, and bounce instead of stretch on the scrolling the app owns (section 6.7).

Ranking: **A (pick)**, then **B** (one capsule, five slots, Start in the exact centre, You moves to the header avatar), then **C** (keep v12's six equal slots).

### 3.1 Platform guidance

| Source | What it says | Use here |
|---|---|---|
| Apple HIG, Tab bars (via sosumi.ai mirror) | Weigh each extra tab against how often it is needed, because fewer are easier (five or fewer as the default where people can customise the bar on iPadOS); avoid an overflow "More" tab, which hides content; one-word labels under icons; **do not use the tab bar for actions**; do not hide or disable tabs, explain empty ones; badges for critical information only. On iOS the bar floats on Liquid Glass; it can minimise into the current tab on scroll; a search tab sits at the trailing end. | Five destinations is the ceiling; Start is an action, so it leaves the tab group; badges stay few. |
| WWDC25 session 284 (via nonstrict.eu index) | The bar floats above content; `tabBarMinimizeBehavior` minimises on scroll in one direction and re-expands on the other; a bottom accessory moves inline when minimised; the trailing search tab expands into a field and collapses the others. | A detached trailing circle next to a capsule is Apple's own Liquid Glass composition. The Live capsule is our bottom accessory. |
| Material 3 navigation bar (`material-components-android` docs) | 3 to 5 destinations, icon plus label; indicator 56 to 64 dp wide x 32 dp, on the icon, with a 4 dp gap to the label; container 80 dp (M3) or 64 dp (Expressive); icon 24 dp; default label rule: with four or more items only the selected one shows its label, "labeled" mode shows all. | Our five destinations sit at M3's ceiling; 64 dp container and 24 dp icons match the Expressive spec. ### 3.3 What the research says for MockMob

- **Count:** five destinations is the ceiling I could confirm (M3 five; HIG says fewer is easier and warns against overflow; Maps three; Spotify three). The owner's 4 to 6 slots is met by five tabs plus a separate key.
- **Where secondary tools go:** apps put them in a hub tab (Maps "You"), the top bar (Maps alerts, Instagram Create), or never in an overflow. MockMob's Colleges is not a secondary tool, it is a destination used weekly with its own pages, so it earns a tab.
- **Actions:** HIG says keep actions out of the tab bar; Spotify's Create shows the cost. Start earns its place because it is daily, so it stays visible, but beside the tabs.

## 5. The decision and its reasons

**Pick A, with the key in the quiet glass style (A), and the lime-filled key (A1) held as the one switch the owner may flip.**

1. **It fixes the measured causes (2.1).** Same-height row, one shared baseline pair, key edges equal capsule edges: this is the literal reading of "perfectly aligned with the bubble and how it's showing things, especially the text" (L31).
2. **It matches the strongest evidence.** Apple's own Liquid Glass bar is a floating capsule with a detached trailing circle (WWDC25 284); HIG says keep actions out of the tab group; M3 caps the bar at five destinations and HIG warns against overflow. A has exactly five destinations and one action.
3. **It meets the owner's count and F2/L22.** Five tabs plus a key is six things (his 4 to 6). Colleges is a named tab, so Compass and My colleges are one tap from the bar; nothing is moved out of sight.
4. **One lime fill per viewport, and no state flipping.** The key's lime is glyph and ring only; the page's own primary stays the only filled lime (AGENT_RULES 7 and 11, S016). A key that follows page state is the exact confusion in F12.
5. **It removes the scrub crossing.** The lens lives only in the capsule.
6. **Thumb reach (my inference, not measured):** Start, the most used control, sits in the right-hand thumb arc.

Risks, stated honestly:
- The owner called the old web Start "popping separately". A is separate by design. It differs from that disc in three ways that the mocks show: same height and baseline as the capsule, same glass material, 8 dp from it, not rising out of it. If he still reads it as separate, B is the answer, and it costs the You tab (Q2).
- The glass key is quieter than the lime pill he has seen for four days. A1 is the lime version. Q1 asks him to choose on the emulator.
- Narrower slots (49.6 at 360). Labels fit (measured above); the build must verify at 1.2 text scale.

Evidence IDs: R7 (one main action), R10 and S014 (do not hide frequent controls), R11 and S016 (consistent navigation), R30 (variants and states), R31 (motion has a purpose), R34 and R36 (verify on small viewports; reduced motion). External: HIG Tab bars, WWDC25 284, Material 3 navigation bar (section 3.1). No A/B result is claimed; none exists.

### 6.2 Surfaces and colour

- Capsule: the existing glass (Night tint `glassTint` 0x9E1C1A16, Day 0xB8FFFDF8), blur sigma 32, plus a saturate of 1.5 on the backdrop (new, Apple-style), 1 dp top edge light (`edgeTop`), soft shadow `glassShadow`. Low glass tier: flat `glassTintLite`, no blur, no saturate.
- Key, resting: the same glass as the capsule, a 1.5 dp ring in `accentText` (lime on Night, `#526700` on Day), a lime play glyph (`accentText`), the word Start in `ink` (weight 700). Capture: `app-A-night.png`, `app-A-day.png`.
- Key variant A1 (owner may choose): filled `volt`, glyph and word in `voltInk`, 1 dp `voltEdge` rim, Night top highlight (as `start_key.dart` today). Captures `app-A1-*`.
- **The key never changes look with the page.** Not on Today, not on Practice, not while a dock or CTA is on screen.
- Tab icons: outline (idle, `ink3`) to filled (active, `ink`); labels `ink3` idle and `ink` active, always weight 600 so nothing shifts on selection. Both must reach 4.5:1 on the glass at the worst content scrolling beneath, in Night and Day, and on the flat tier.
- Lens: `litTile` fill with a 1 dp top edge light. Never lime.
- Badges: Review keeps its count; You and Colleges keep their dots (IA 4.4). Because HIG reserves badges for critical information, show at most two badges or dots at once, and the Review count wins ties. Position unchanged (centre at icon right +2, icon top +1).
- One blur layer: draw the capsule and the key inside one `BackdropGroup` so the two shapes share a single backdrop capture (A1 needs no second blur). Verify the API against the Flutter version in the build packet.

### 6.4 States

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Loading | The bar renders at once with no badges; counts arrive later and pop in (badge scale 0.8 to 1, 220 ms spring). Nothing waits for data. |
| Empty / new account | No Review count; no dots; Colleges and You open their own empty states. The bar is identical to any other account. |
| Offline | The live capsule above the bar says "You are offline" (existing). The bar is unchanged. |
| Sparse | Nothing is hidden or greyed: a tab is never disabled because it is empty (HIG). |
| Success (a paper just finished) | Review's count pops from N to N+k (220 ms). The lens does not move. |
| Return | Cold start opens Today with the lens on Today; the key shows Resume if a paper is open. |
| Compact (scrolled down 24 dp) | Capsule 52 tall (band y 12 to 64 of the row), key 52 circle, both bottom-anchored so their centres move together to y 38; labels fade out over 120 ms; icons and the key glyph move down 15 dp; lens 44 tall (y 16 to 60). Restored by 8 dp of upward scroll or a tap. 240 ms, `Motion.emphasized` out, `Motion.glide` back (existing curves). |
| Keyboard open | The bar and key hide together (existing rule). |
| Full-screen pages (runner, result, repair, Compass, Combo) | Cover the bar, as today. Nothing animates in a timed paper. |

### 6.5 Interaction and motion

All springs are expressed as damping ratio and response, after Apple's model (critically damped by default, bounce only after a flick).

| Interaction | Behaviour | Duration and curve |
|---|---|---|
| Tab press | Slot scales to 0.96 on pointer down (response on down, not on release). | 100 ms ease-out; release spring 260 ms (existing). |
| Tab tap | Lens travels to the slot with no overshoot; the old icon morphs fill to outline, the new outline to fill; selection haptic. | Spring damping 1.0, response 0.30 s (about 320 ms to settle). Icon morph 200 ms in, 160 ms out (existing). |
| Lens grab (new) | Finger down anywhere on the capsule lifts the lens: scale 1.0 to 1.08 and its top edge light from 12% to 30%. | 120 ms ease-out in, 200 ms out. |
| Lens drag | The lens follows the finger 1:1 keeping the grab offset, stretched along the drag by up to 1.12 with velocity (existing `lean`). An icon within one slot of the lens centre magnifies up to 1.06 (labels never scale). Selection haptic at each slot boundary. | Tracking has no duration; magnify is a function of distance. |
| Lens release | Project the end point with `d = 0.998` (`(v/1000) x d / (1 - d)`), snap to the nearest slot centre of the projection, hand the finger velocity to the spring. | Spring damping 0.8, response 0.30 s; overshoot at most 4%. |
| Lens at the ends | Past Today or You it resists with a rubber band (`o x dim x 0.55 / (dim + 0.55 x |o|)`, dim = 1.2 slots). | Follows the finger; release spring as above. |
| Key press | Scales to 0.94; ring goes to 2 dp; fill takes 12% lime (A) or deepens 16% (A1). Release overshoots a little and an ink ring spreads out of it (existing). | 100 ms down; ring 380 ms (existing). |
| Key drag up | Stretches tall and narrow, volume kept, anchored at its foot; the Start sheet morphs out of the key's rect (existing; origin is now a circle). | Existing. |
| Scroll minimise | Compact on 24 dp down, restore on 8 dp up (above). Both the capsule and the key move on one controller so they never disagree. | 240 ms out, glide back. |
| Badge change | Pop 0.8 to 1. | 220 ms spring. |

Reduced motion (the app's setting or Android "remove animations"): no lens lift, magnify, stretch, rubber band or overshoot; the lens jumps with a 120 ms cross-fade; compact state by a 120 ms cross-fade; key press is a colour change only; haptics stay. Nothing essential is carried by motion alone: selection is also shown by icon fill, ink colour and the lens.

### 6.7 Liquid Glass at the app level (F3)

What carries over, and what cannot:

| Apple behaviour | In the app | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Floating glass capsule over content | Yes (the capsule, sigma 32, saturate 1.5, edge light). | Content scrolls under it; clearance already exists (`MmBar.clearanceAbove`). |
| Lens that follows and magnifies | Yes (6.5). | Magnify is a per-icon transform, cheap. |
| Minimise on scroll, accessory moves inline | Yes (compact state; the live capsule sinks as it does today). | |
| Detached trailing circle | Yes (the key). | |
| True optical refraction of what is under the glass | **No.** A fragment shader that bends the backdrop is possible with Impeller but costs real frame time on a 2 to 4 GB phone. Not specified; if the owner wants it, build it behind the existing glass-quality tier and measure jank first. | |
| Bounce instead of Android's stretch (his words: "we can control the app UI") | Yes, for scrolling the app owns: one `ScrollBehavior` on the `MaterialApp` (`lib/app.dart`, which has none today) returning bouncing physics for tab roots and sheets and no stretch indicator. ### 6.8 The one rule that stops the Start key flipping (F12 and the dock)
