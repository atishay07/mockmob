### 2.3 Rate limits (`http.js:22-33`, persistent, 429 with Retry-After and a sentence naming the real wait)
read 120 per 10 min; write 60 per hour; invite link 5 per day; lookup 120 per 10 min per IP; join 10 per hour (successes only); report 10 per day; start a Mob 3 per week (successes); extra paper 6 per day (successes). Invite link life 7 days (`INVITE_DAYS`), uses = seats left (max 4). Extras: 3 per Mob per week, lengths 5/10/15/20.

### 2.4 What one member can see about another (privacy; `buildView` `model.mjs:343+`, `compare.mjs` header)
- Always: name, colour, join order, owner flag, whether they sat this week (every member is listed with `sat: bool`; copy never counts or names non-sitters, rule P6), how many sat. Never an id, email, photo, marks, streak or last-active time.
- Another member's score only if they share (`show_score`, on by default, one switch); your own is always yours.
- Per-question picks, seconds, changed answers of another member: only when BOTH of you share. A member who keeps theirs private is counted ("1 member keeps their answers private"), never named, never shown as a gap. Plan gate: full side-by-side is Pro; Free gets one sample and the one-line teaser.
- Shared trap: after you sat, 3+ sitters, option picked by 2+ and half+, aggregate only. "Together": a count among sitters who all share. Teaser needs 3 sitters or every sitter sharing.
- Nudge: only the sender's name, only to recipients who have not sat; no read receipt back.
- Report/leave/block/remove: report stores reason + reported name (moderators see only that); "Leave and block" removes you and stores a block so you never share a Mob again (`mob_block`); only the owner can remove (the member needs a new link); leaving deletes your sittings in that Mob. A bad code and a block read identically (P10).
- Not built by rule (`DOCS/design-decisions/pass-1016e/SOCIAL-SPEC.md` section 11): chat, DMs, comments, reactions, images, links, free text beyond the name, public profiles, member search, contacts, leagues, ranks, score gaps, losable streaks, countdowns, shame copy, friend-invite credits, push in v1, Mob for timed full mocks.

### 2.6 Truthful social signals
- Exist in Mob tables: who sat this week; scores (if shared); per-question picks, seconds, changes (if both share); past weeks' sat counts and your own score (Pro); extras and who sat them; nudge sent/seen; join order; join and leave times; invite uses.
- Exist elsewhere, not shown to other members: per-user attempts and answers (`attempts`, `WEB/supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql`); personal rhythm (days practised, `weeksMetStreak`, goal met; `WEB/src/lib/server/rhythm.js`, `/api/me/rhythm`, `/api/me/goal`, `study_goals`); 16 personal milestone badges (`/api/me/badges`); Your standing (anonymous band).
- Do not exist: "who practised today", any live or per-day activity per member, a Mob streak, avatars (colour only), reactions, quests or shared targets, head-to-head, timed races, last-active, push.
- Would need new tables or columns (additive): `mob_members.avatar` (cheap); a daily activity flag per member with its own sharing switch; `mob_reactions` (fixed preset enum); `mob_quests`/progress (computable from sittings plus attempts); challenge records (or reuse `mob_extras`); for live play a room table plus a Realtime channel with auth; a push sender plus `firebase_messaging`.

## 3. Mobi: how he is drawn (`APP/lib/core/mascot/`)
- Shape grammar: a square of paper folded into a slightly tilted diamond that is the whole head and body, with a dark visor, two capsule eyes, one antenna with a bulb, black rubber-hose arms ending in big mittens, short stub legs and big boots. No mouth, nose or ears. Expression lives in the lids (a sloped upper lid is the brow), a warm lower lid, gaze, antenna and posture (`mobi_mood.dart`: calm, happy, proud, curious, sleepy, concerned, focused).
- Geometry in design units, box 140 x 168, soles on y 162 (`mobi_geometry.dart`). Body diamond: top (75,30), right (126,84), bottom (71,142), left (16,79); corner radii 9/16/13/16; sides bulge 6 outward (soft, pillowy paper); drawn 4 below; pivot (71,136). So the body is about 110 wide by 112 tall, about 0.8 of the box width. A lower-right fold with a crease (`mobi_parts.dart` `_body`: fold gradient, `limeHi` 1.3 crease line), lamp sheen at upper left, soft shade toward the bottom corner where the legs go in.
- Visor: rounded rectangle 66 x 34, radius 16, centre (70,82), recessed lip, vertical gradient `2B2926` > `151413` > `0B0A09`, a curved gloss band across the top (white 20% fading to 0). Eyes: capsules 7.6 x 15, centres 13.5 either side of the visor centre and 1.5 down, colour `FFFAEE` (shade `D9D0BD`); eye state `open, lid, smile, slant`; gaze travels up to (9, 4.5); the two eyes can differ.
- Antenna: base (90,50), tip (104,9), black stalk, ball radius 5.8; the bulb lights faintly for think, focus and proud (a glow at Night, a 1 dp `#526700` edge by day).
- Limbs: shoulders (35,104) and (108,103); arm length 23, width 9.6; mitten scale 1.22; hips (62,131) and (81,131); leg width 9.6; ankles (47,142.5) and (96,142.5); boots scale 1.3, toe toward +x, a cuff lip over the leg. - Proportion: no separate head; legs are stubs; big hands and boots, small limbs; overall about 1 wide to 1.2 tall.

## 6. Build constraints

### Cheap now (existing tables and routes; the server stays authoritative)
- **Avatar characters**: one additive column (`mob_members.avatar smallint`, a migration) or a field on the existing `PATCH /api/mob/me` and `MobMember` model; the art is client-side (the Mobi rig and its `head` crop can draw a family; no network).
- **Async challenges on existing tables**: `mob_extras` + `mob_extra_sittings` already give a paper of 5/10/15/20 questions on a chapter or the whole subject, open 7 days, sat by any member, with side-by-side, no credits, 3 per Mob per week, Pro to make. A "sprint" or "custom length" is a policy change (who may make them, the 3 per week cap, `EXTRA_LENGTHS`) plus UI, not a new table. A "10 minutes" paper is runner config (60 s a question today, `RUNNER_SECONDS_PER_QUESTION`).
- **Display from data already sent**: a lobby view can be built from `GET /api/mob` as is (names, colours, who sat, shared scores, sat count); ring, sitter discs, members, side-by-side, trap, together, history are all data-complete.
- **Local-only delight**: Mobi acts, avatar celebrations, ring ink, haptics, sound; each frame-checked per R5.
- Small additive server work (hours): preset-sticker reactions (`mob_reactions`, fixed enum, no free text); a weekly Mob target computed from `mob_sittings` plus extras; a shared week streak computed from consecutive weeks where every member sat (derivable today; a design call because it can be lost, T005).

### Needs a privacy or owner decision first
- "Who practised today": needs a per-member daily flag from `attempts` and a sharing switch; today non-sitters are never shown (P6) and the nudge is silent on who has not sat. Named nudges ("Riya is waiting") reverse that.
- Leagues, ranking, head-to-head: rejected in SOCIAL-SPEC 12 and P5 "never ranked"; his 19 Oct direction invites them back; minors' names must stay inside the Mob.

### Expensive
- **Live rooms, simultaneous race, presence**: nothing exists. Needs Supabase Realtime with authenticated access (all Mob tables are service-role only with no client policy, so table Realtime is blocked; a Broadcast channel authorised by the API would be new), a room table and lifecycle, reconnect and cheat handling, server-timed questions (today the runner keeps a local draft and `/api/mob/paper/start` returns a session to run). Egress and moderation cost rise; the Mob today calls no AI model and costs about nothing.
- **Push**: needs `firebase_messaging` (or equivalent), `google-services.json`, an FCM sender job and prefs (the token route, `push_devices`, `notification_prefs` and the null `PushTransport` seam exist). Until then "ask the others" reaches people only at their next app open.
- **Web parity**: `WEB/src/components/mob/*` must follow the app's pick (PLAN-1019-PM).
- **Process limits**: no production writes, deploys or paid steps without his say (D122). The Mob flag is already ON in production, so any schema change goes through a migration and the staging gate. Tests that lock current copy and shapes: `WEB/data/tests/mob.test.mjs`, `APP/test/` (`mob_tab_test`, social goldens).
