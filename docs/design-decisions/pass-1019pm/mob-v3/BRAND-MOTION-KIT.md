# Mob brand and motion kit (19 Oct 2026, pass-1019pm, mob-v3)

Purpose: make the Mob look and move like MockMob and nothing else. Owner brief (4:38 and 4:42 pm): premium, refined, gamified, "motion design plays a crucial role", "give it a brand identity of Mockmob". Everything below is built from the repo (colours from `mm_colors.dart`, motion from `motion_tokens.g.dart`, Mobi from `mascot/`, art from `assets/art`). The owner's research rules still hold: truthful numbers, no shame, no fake activity, collectibles earned and never bought.

## 0. What the research did and did not confirm
Searches gave thin detail on rival internals, so each claim is tagged. (S) = stated by a page I read. (C) = standard animation and game-feel craft that I am applying; it is not a claim about that app.
- Duolingo (S): milestone days make Duo "physically change", framed as a game power-up; the team iterated rough passes on rhythm and said timing matters as much as the design; the first balloon version was "cute" but not energetic; a share card extends the moment. Duolingo hires motion designers for streak, lesson and leaderboard moments, and its Rive case study says a state machine handles transitions and blending; one designer says every animation needs a "beat" even without sound. Why it works (C): the reward is a change in the character, not a popup, so it feels earned.
- Clash Royale and Brawl Stars (S, thin): a Core Games brief cites Hearthstone, where an unopened card glows in its rarity colour, so anticipation is built before the reveal. The pacing analysis (one chest at a time, four slots, timers) is what we reject: timers and queues are the pressure and gambling-adjacent part. Keep the anticipation craft (C): pull-back, wobble, light leak, burst, settle. Never a random draw.
- Kahoot (S, thin): lobby music, a top-three podium, sound can be muted from the screen, and hosts asked for a one-at-a-time podium reveal back. So build-up and a mute control are what audiences want. (C): third, second, first order gives suspense.
- Apple rings (S, thin): a "fireworks" moment when a ring closes; no Apple haptic spec was found. (C): the ring closing is a number made visible and the light arrives exactly when the shape completes.
- Revolut and HIGH: no verified teardown found. (C) Principle only: confirm a real state change with one small, precise motion plus one haptic, never decoration.
- Game feel (S, practitioner blogs): "Juice it or lose it" layers flash, shake, particles, sound; hit-stop of about 50 to 100 ms; shake with decaying amplitude; a counterpoint warns that juice for its own sake costs focus. We take: few layers, each doing a job, every layer cuttable.
- Flutter (S): custom fragment shaders work on Skia and Impeller; `ImageFilter.shader` is Impeller only; precache programs before an animation; no vertex shaders, no UBOs, `drawImage` ignores the shader. Rive says its files are 10 to 15 times smaller than Lottie (vendor claim, unverified by me).
Principles used in every moment: anticipation, overshoot and settle, squash and stretch, secondary motion (cloth, dust, light), staging (one hero per beat), timing on a shared beat, sound and haptic on the same frame as the visual contact.

## 1. Brand rules: the Mob is MockMob
One sentence: a lamp-lit exam desk where friends sit with folded paper, and the volt light is switched on for whoever earned it.
**Signature elements (use at least three on every Mob screen):**
1. Paper: folded diamond silhouettes (Mobi's family), creases, a corner fold, paper tile surfaces (`paper-tile.webp`), pillowy 6-unit edge bulge. Things arrive by unfolding and leave by folding (`fold_exit.dart`).
2. Stamps and seals: results are stamped, not popped (`foil-seal.webp`, `token-*.webp`). A stamp lands with scale 1.4 to 1, a 2 degree settle and a light bloom.
3. Lamp light: warm tungsten pool from the top (`lamp F2A93B`), the one moving light source. Reveals begin with a light change, not a particle.
4. Volt: the single lit element per screen or moment: the primary key, the winner's light, the new stitch. Never two volt lights at once, never on letters in the logo.
5. Exam stationery: OMR bubbles, answer-sheet ticks, graph-paper grid at 4% ink, ruler ticks, bulldog clip, desk bell, stopwatch, metronome, compass (`hybrid-*.webp`). Use them as UI (the bell is the reward sound, the stopwatch is the countdown, the metronome is the Mob beat).
6. Lowercase `mockmob` wordmark with Mobi as the second o appears at least once per share card and loading splash, never inside the game.
**Never (the Mob must not read as Kahoot, Duolingo or Clash):** coins, gems, hearts, chests, loot boxes, XP bars with a "level", rainbow confetti, purple or neon gradients, Material ripples, emoji in UI, glass blur on game surfaces (glass stays in the dock and sheets), ranking below third, red alarms, shake on a wrong answer, "you are losing" copy, countdown to lose a streak.
**Two tests before a screen ships:** (a) cover the logo: could this be another app? If yes, add a paper, stamp or lamp element. (b) one-lit-thing test: point at the brightest thing; it must be the thing the student should do or just earned.
Characters: the generated 3D paper folk (separate brief, mob-v3 characters). They are folded-paper toys with Mobi's visor-and-capsule-eye grammar, lamp-lit, one raster per character per mood; animation is done around the still (section 6, option C). Lore hook for marketing: a Mob is a study group of paper folk who share one desk; the lamp stays on while anyone is still working. Story lives in names, the den and the stamp, not in cutscenes.

## 3. Type rules
- Figures (marks, rank numeral, streak number, countdown, quest count): `BricolageCondensed` Bold, tabular, with `figureHeightBehavior`. Countdown 96/86 (figureHero), marks 52/47, streak number 52.
- Moment titles ("Quest done", "Week 4"): `BricolageHeadline` 28/31 or 36/38 once per moment. Screen titles: `BricolageTitle` 22/27. Everything a student reads (names, captions, buttons, rules): Hanken Grotesk, names 17/22 w700, caption 13/18.
- Max two sizes in any moment; sentence case; no outline, italics, gradients on text, or letter-spaced capitals. First names only, 2 to 18 characters, never truncated (wrap to two lines on the podium before shrinking below 13).
- Motion for text: numbers roll (`numberRoll` 500 ms) and settle; words engrave left to right (`engrave` 280 ms); nothing moves after landing.
- Copy voice: plain, warm, grammatical, no exclamation marks, no telegram style. Praise names the real thing: "You and Riya got question 6 right." Wrong answer: "Not this one. It was B." Never "lost", "behind" or "last".

## 4. Iconography
- Utility icons: `lucide_icons_flutter`, 2 dp stroke, round caps, 24 dp in a 48 dp target; filled variant only for the selected state.
- Mob glyphs, custom painted in one weight: bubble (OMR), tick, pennant flag, stamp, desk bell, stopwatch, metronome, clip, parcel (folded envelope). Filled glyphs have a one-fold corner and a lamp-lit top edge, nothing else.
- Reactions: a fixed preset set of paper stickers (no free text, no photos, no emoji glyphs because they change per phone). Sent as an enum, drawn locally.
- Mobi appears as host in set moments only (parcel open, quest stamp, empty states). The old "once per screen" rule is relaxed to once per moment inside the Mob, per the owner's all-out brief.

## 5. Sound and haptics
**Sound (new; there is no audio package in `pubspec.yaml` today, so this needs an owner nod, a package choice and about 15 clips).** Palette: wood and paper. Metronome tick for the Mob beat, woodblock for selection, a paper flip for a friend finishing, a stopwatch tick for the countdown, a brass desk bell for first place and quest done, a low soft "pat" for a wrong answer. Rules: each clip 80 ms to 1.4 s; all ≤ 400 KB together compressed; on by default only while a game is open; silent when the phone is on silent or vibrate (needs a check through the existing haptics method channel; not yet verified); ducks, never stops, the student's music; a Mob sound switch in Mob settings and in You; sound never carries information that is not also visible. Pitch story: a right answer rises a whole tone from the previous right answer up to three, then resets; a wrong answer never descends or buzzes.
**Haptics: reuse the existing grammar in `haptics.dart`, add no verbs.** `selection` for selecting, a tab or a sticker; `light` for the key commit, Go, and each podium landing; `medium` for the seal landing and first place; `confirm` only after the server acknowledged (podium final, quest done, streak extended); `toggle` for the podium switch. Nothing on a wrong answer. Nothing when a friend does something (no buzzing for others). Nothing on opening a sheet.

## 6. Motion language
**Principles.** (1) One clock: the Mob beat is 600 ms (100 beats a minute, the metronome). Show moments and idle loops use multiples of a beat or a half beat; UI chrome keeps the app's tokens (press 100, release 140, card 240, enter 360, emphasized 420 ms, stagger 45 ms). (2) Light first: every moment opens with a light change 120 to 300 ms before anything moves. (3) Characters get squash and stretch and bounce; chrome does not (`Springs.celebrate` 520 ms bounce 0.22 is allowed for Mob characters and seals, because they are Mobi's family; everything else uses `smooth`, `snappy`, `iconPop`, `settle`). (4) Paper physics: unfold, stamp, slide, flip; never spin, never scale from 0. (5) At most three things move at once; one hero per beat. (6) Interruptible: input is never blocked, any tap skips a show moment to its final state in 200 ms. (7) Truth: animation reveals data already settled on the server; it never invents progress, and a result is shown only after the server acknowledged it.
**New tokens (add to `shared/motion-tokens.json`, then regenerate; the web reads the same file):** `mob.beat` 600 ms; a ladder tier T4 `momentShow` ceiling 6000 ms for the podium only (T2 1200 and T3 2000 stay for the rest, enforced by tests); `mob.squash` 0.88 x and 1.08 y for 90 ms on `press`/`out`. Reuse every other token.
**Common layer stack (back to front):** ground and grain, lamp light field, den floor, character shadow (ellipse, 18% black), character art, type, particles (paper bits, glints), chrome. Each layer is its own `RepaintBoundary`; only transform and opacity animate, except the light field and shaders.
**Particles.** Paper bits (4 to 8 dp rectangles with a fold line, rotation and flutter), four-point glints (reuse `EmblemSparkle`'s glint), a ring burst. Palette: paper white, lamp, volt, the Mob's member colours. Cap 60 alive, lifetime 600 to 1400 ms, gravity plus 12% drag. No particle ever crosses text.

### Signature moments (names are the ones the specs and tests use)
Format: timeline in ms; curve or spring; layers; reduced motion (RM). Haptic and sound on the same frame as the cause.
**1. Enter the Den (T2, 1100).** 0: ground fades in on `emphasized`. 0 to 420: lamp pool grows from 0 to full (`glide`). 120: floor and table rise 14 dp. 200 + 45 per friend (cap 5): each character rises 14 dp and scales 0.92 to 1 on `iconPop`, then 12 dp of paper-shadow settles. 700: idle starts: a 1200 ms breath (scale y 1.00 to 1.02), each phase offset 120 ms, one blink per 4 to 7 s; loops stop 10 s after the last touch and when the tab is hidden. Haptic none (the tap already ticked). Sound none. RM: 120 ms cross-fade, still frames, light fixed.
**2. Go live (T2, 900).** Key press 0.96 on `snappy`; on release a volt wipe leaves the key across the stage in 360 ms (`out`), then fades to a 6% volt wash. Characters turn 4 degrees toward the centre (240 ms). Each friend who joins hops in (560 ms, the Pip hop) with a "Here" tag that swaps in on `engrave`; only real presence is shown. Haptic `light` at release; `selection` per joiner. Sound: woodblock per joiner. RM: wipe becomes a 120 ms fade, no hop.
**3. Countdown (3.0 s).** Beats 600 ms: 3, 2, 1, then Go at 1800 to 2400. Each numeral: stamp in 0 to 180 ms (scale 1.25 to 1 on `out`), hold, leave 140 ms on `leave` (scale 0.92, fade). Behind it a paper tile tilts 1.5 degrees per beat like a metronome arm. Scrim rises 0 to 45% across the three beats (the lamp narrows toward the question). On Go: numeral turns volt fill (voltInk on Day), one ring burst 0 to 1.6 scale, fade, 420 ms, then the question rises 14 dp on `glide`. Haptic `selection` on 3, 2, 1; `light` on Go. Sound: stopwatch tick x3, then a bright woodblock. RM: numerals cross-fade in place (120 ms); the three beats stay, because the time is information.
**4. Answer lock-in (T1, 320).** Tap: selection haptic, option presses to 0.96 (100 ms) and its lip sinks 2 dp. "Lock in" key commit: a paper clip drops 8 dp onto the option corner in 140 ms on `snappy`, the other options dim to 60% in 160 ms, your character nods 3 degrees (240 ms). In live play your friends' name tags show a small pennant only when they lock in, never what they chose. Haptic `light` on lock. Sound: woodblock. RM: clip appears, dim in 120 ms, no nod.
**5. Right and wrong feedback (T1, 560).** Right: option fills `goodText` tint in 200 ms, a volt tick draws on its stroke (`drawIn` 450 ms, leading at 120), "+5" floats up 12 dp and fades over 700 ms (CUET marks; ink2, tabular), the character does a 420 ms nod-hop. Glint at the tick, nothing else. Wrong: option takes `wrongSurface` and `wrongBorder` in 200 ms, the right option draws its tick, "-1" shows in ink2 (small, not red), the character tilts 6 degrees and returns in 420 ms ("hmm", never sad, never asleep). No shake, no screen flash, no buzzer. Right-in-a-row: after 3, one extra glint, no counter that can break and be mourned. Haptic: `light` on right (live games only), none on wrong. Sound: right = rising woodblock up to three, wrong = soft pat. RM: tint and tick appear in 120 ms; no float, no nod.
**6. A friend finishing (T1, 600).** Their character raises a paper pennant (scale y 0 to 1 from the pole, 280 ms on `iconPop`), the pennant takes their member colour, a page-flip sound plays, and their row slides into the progress strip (`glide` 300 ms). No haptic. Never shows their correctness or a gap to you. RM: pennant fades in over 120 ms.
**7. Podium reveal (T4, about 4800).** 0 to 400: scrim to 55%, lamp pool narrows to the podium (`emphasized`). 400: three blocks unfold from flat (rotateX 90 to 0, 420 ms, `emphasized`) in order third, second, first, one beat apart; a block with a tie shares one place. Each character drops 60 dp, squashes (x 1.08, y 0.88, 90 ms) and settles on `celebrate`; 10 paper bits puff on contact. Marks roll up with `countUp` 720 ms beside each name; place numeral engraves 280 ms later. First place (at 2200): lamp pool swells to 60%, foil sheen sweeps the block (`sweep` 700 ms, shader or a gradient mask), 40 paper bits and 8 glints burst for 1400 ms, the bell rings, the character does one hop. Fourth and fifth stand on the floor beside the podium with names, no numeral, no shading. Switch off ("Show a podium"): all characters land together on the table with marks and no place. A tap skips to the final state in 200 ms. Haptic `light` on each landing, `medium` on first, `confirm` after the server ack. Sound: three rising woodblock notes, then the bell. RM: the final standing fades in over 120 ms, no particles, no sheen; the order of reveal is kept as three 120 ms fades 300 ms apart (sequence is information).
**8. Streak extending (T3, 1800).** The weekly Mob streak is a stitched paper pennant: one volt stitch per week in a row (max 12 drawn, then a number). Quest done leads here. 0: pennant lifts 8 dp. 180: the number rolls (`numberRoll` 500) from n-1 to n. 300: a needle-thread draws the new stitch along the border (`drawIn` 450, volt). 800: pennant gives a 1.03 settle (`lift`), a lamp glint rides the stitch. Haptic `confirm` after the ack. Sound: two bell notes. Missed week: no motion and no warning; the next week the pennant quietly cross-fades to "Week 1" and "Best run 5" stays on the shelf. No countdown, no droop, no loss copy. RM: number cross-fades, stitch appears.
**9. Character reveal (T3, 2000), earned only.** The parcel shows its real silhouette on the quest card in advance, so nothing is a mystery draw. 0: parcel sits, lamp narrows. On "Open": pull-back (scale 0.94, 120 ms), two 3 degree wobbles (2 x 160 ms), then four flaps unfold 60 ms apart over 420 ms with light leaking from the seam (lamp 0 to 90 to 50%). The character rises 40 dp with `celebrate`, shadow grows, name engraves (280 ms), a bell note. Haptic `light` at pull-back, `medium` at open. Share card: a still render of the same layers on a paper tile with the lowercase wordmark, for social posts. RM: parcel cross-fades to the character in 120 ms.
**10. Quest completing (T3, 1900).** The quest bar is split by member colour. Last tick fills; segments bloom in order with 45 ms steps (each lifts 1 dp and glows 14%); the bar converts to the foil seal, which stamps in on `celebrate` (scale 1.4 to 1, 2 degree settle, 520 ms) with a 12-glint ring and light bloom (EmblemSparkle pattern); "Quest done" in headline engraves; then the parcel arrives at 1400 (moment 9 cue). Haptic `medium` at seal landing, `confirm` after ack. Sound: bell. RM: bar fills and the seal fades in at 120 ms.
**Small ones.** Press: scale 0.96, `snappy`. Sticker send: sticker flies 24 dp to the character and rises, 360 ms, sound none. Switch Mobs: fade-through 90 and 210 ms. Idle: characters breathe only inside the den, nowhere else.

## 7. Reduced motion, cost tiers and checks
- Reduced motion (system or in-app override via `Motion.reduced`): fewer and gentler, not none. 120 ms cross-fades, no particles, no sheen, no squash, loops stopped, light fixed. Information, order and sound stay. Haptics and sound have their own switches. "Skip" is always visible on show moments.
- Quality tiers follow `glass_quality.dart`: Full as above; Lite halves particles, drops shaders and the light-field blur. Budgets: 60 fps target; at most 60 particles, 1 shader and 1 blur per moment, one image sequence in memory at a time; test on the emulator at 2 to 3 GB (owner's RAM rule) and on a low-end phone, because many of the 16 to 17-year-old students will not own a flagship (assumption, to confirm with real devices).
- Every moment gets a frame strip (R5) and a duration assertion against its tier ceiling; the podium test asserts the 6000 ms ceiling and that the skip lands in 200 ms.

## 8. What Flutter can render well, and the cost
| Option | Good for | Cost | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Implicit and explicit animations plus the app's springs | press, enter, stagger, number roll, stamps | none; already the app's engine | base layer for all 10 moments |
| B. `CustomPainter` (particles, rings, stitch, bar, pennant) | paper bits, glints, ring burst, quest bar, drawn stitch | CPU per frame; cap 60 particles; one ticker; paint-only, so no layout | yes; the Mobi rig (12k lines) shows the team can do it |
| C. Still raster plus transform puppet (the generated characters) | breathe, nod, hop, squash, tilt, rise from a parcel, on 24 characters | cheap: one image per mood; poses are a loss of expression vs a rig | primary character method; pair with a drawn shadow ellipse and a rim-light sweep |
| D. Fragment shaders (`mm_glass.frag` precedent) | foil sheen, light bloom, paper-fold shading, lamp pool | GLSL limits (no `drawImage`, no vertex stage); precache before use; `ImageFilter.shader` Impeller only; Lite tier off | yes for sheen and light only, at most 3 shaders |
| E. Image sequence (animated WebP or sprite sheet) | parcel flap unfold, one-shot reveals | 12 frames at 360 px about 6 MB decoded; 24 characters at 24 frames would not fit; frames from a generator drift between frames | only for one-shots, decoded on demand and evicted |
| F. Rive (state machine, events) | rigged looping characters with inputs; Duolingo's pattern | new dependency and runtime, `.riv` authored in Rive's editor (nobody on the team has shipped one here), rigging per character; vendor says files are 10 to 15 times smaller | not for build 1; revisit for a shared character rig if the puppet method feels flat |
| G. Lottie | linear After Effects pieces | After Effects pipeline, JSON size, rasterised blur and shadow, no state | no |
Recommended path: A + B + C everywhere, D for foil and light, E only for the parcel; sound and haptics from section 5. All of it is local, with no network and no AI at runtime.
