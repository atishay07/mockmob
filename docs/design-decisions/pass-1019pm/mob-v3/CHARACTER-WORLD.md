## 1. The world, in five lines

1. There is one endless strip of paper, folded once so it has only one side: the Long Paper. Every mock in MockMob is cut from it, which is why the mocks never run out.
2. Every mistake leaves a crease in the paper. Left alone, a crease hardens into a trap.
3. When someone fixes a mistake and the fix holds on a later check, the crease smooths out and a small paper creature unfolds from it. They are called the Folds.
4. Mobi was the first Fold, from the very first fixed mistake, so he is the eldest. He knows every bend of the Long Paper and shows the way.
5. Each Fold remembers the habit it came from (rushing, second-guessing, guessing on minus marks) and is a little bit like that still. That is why students recognise themselves in them.

So every character is a fixed mistake with a face. The product promise (find where marks slip, fix it, check the fix holds) is the origin story, and nothing in the lore is false about the app.
The lamp: a Mob is a desk where friends sit with their Folds. The lamp stays on while anyone is still working (from the brand kit).

Lines for marketing, in the owner's voice: "Every mistake you fix leaves something behind." "Meet the Folds. Each one was a mistake once." "Which Fold are you?"

## 3. How a student meets them

- **First fold.** When a student joins their first Mob, Mobi hands them a sealed paper parcel. It unfolds into the starter they pick from four (all four shown first, so nothing is hidden or random). This replaces the old character picker as the first moment.
- **The shelf.** Every Fold has a slot from day one. Empty slots are a dashed paper outline with the true rule written under it ("Fix five mistakes that stay fixed"). Progress shows as real counts ("3 of 5"). No silhouettes to tease, no countdowns.
- **The Unfold (reveal).** When a rule is met, the parcel appears on the next calm screen (never mid-mock). The lamp brightens, the seal pops, the flaps open, the Fold peeks out, steps out and does its signature pose. About 2.4 s, tap skips in 200 ms, reduced motion is a crossfade (brand kit moment "character reveal"). The surprise is the look and the line, never whether you get something.
- **In the Mob.** Your Fold is your seat at the desk: lobby, ghost race marker, podium figure, sticker. Mob-earned Folds (Crane, Stamp) unfold for every member at once, on the shared shelf.
- **Kept for good.** Never lost, never decays, kept after leaving a Mob. Not tradable, not giftable, not convertible, not for sale.

### Variants (finishes), all from hard true events

Each Fold has four finishes after the plain one. The rule is printed on the locked finish. Rare means hard to do, never lucky.
1. **Plain** (lamp-lit paper): on unfolding.
2. **Graph paper**: play 10 Mob games with this Fold as your seat.
3. **Glow ink** (glow-in-the-dark like Smiski, lime on Night): your Mob's weekly streak reaches 3 weeks.
4. **Foil edge** (lamp-gold foil on the folds): beat this Fold's own habit, the "last page". Dart: finish a mock on pace with fewer wrong answers than your last. Dog-ear: a mock where none of your changed answers went from right to wrong. Tally: a mock with no negative marks. Patang: three subjects improved. Sticky: 20 fixes held. Hexa: three chapters complete. Kandil: three full mocks. Cutting: a month of weekly practice after a break. Hop: a right answer straight after a wrong one, ten times in one mock. Naav: the last ten minutes of a mock with no wrong answers. Crane and Stamp: their quest done twice.
5. **Pencil sketch** (a hand-drawn version, the craft signal): finish all three above for that Fold.

## 4. Five social media ideas

1. **The Unfold** reels: 6 to 8 second clips of a parcel opening into a Fold, cut from the in-app animation (no extra cost). One Fold per post, with its line: "Dart. Answers first, then learns to check."
2. **"Which Fold are you?"** A WhatsApp sticker pack of the 12 Folds (signature poses plus five reactions each) and a carousel of habits. Students send it to the friend who is that Fold. Stickers are the free, safe way the cast travels in India.
3. **Exam-season drop.** In the week after board exams end, a new Fold from the reserve (for example Admit, the admit card) joins the Long Paper, earned by the first full mock after boards. Tease it truthfully on Instagram with the real date, and never claim a crowd.
4. **Fold your own Dart.** A free printable paper-plane sheet with Dart's visor printed on it, plus a reel of the fold. It turns classrooms into marketing, and it is paper craft, so it is the brand.
5. **Desk tours.** A shareable shelf card (first name optional, no photo) and a short series in a dry, kind character voice: "Dart vs the 72-second rule", "Dog-ear changes its answer again", "Cutting takes a break, and that is allowed". No menace, no streak guilt.

## 5. Style comparison and recommendation

Sheet: `tmp/design-1019pm/mob-v3/characters/STYLES-compare.png`. It shows the same four Folds (Dart, Dog-ear, Kandil, Hop) in three styles, at Night and Day, with the same prompt, framing and light.

| | A: soft glossy sticker 3D (our take on Genmoji) | B: collectible vinyl toy (blind-box figure on a base) | C: lamp-lit folded paper (Mobi's house style) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What it looks like | Puffed, satin-gloss volumes. Bright and emoji-clear. | The same figures as painted vinyl toys on a charcoal base with a lime ring. | Matte card paper with fibre and crisp folds, lit warm by the lamp. The visor is the only shiny part. |
| Belongs with Mobi and the 30 subject objects | Close, but the gloss makes Mobi look like a different family. | The figures belong, but the base sets them apart from Mobi. | Yes. It is the same hand as `mobi-*.webp` and `obj-*.webp`. |
| Reads at 40 px (disc, sticker) | Best, because it has the most contrast. | Weakest, because the base takes about 20% of the height. | Good. The silhouettes and the visor carry it. |
| Craft signal (research: imperfect beats AI-smooth) | Low. The gloss is what reads as "AI 3D". | Medium. The paint and seam details feel made. | High. Fibre, creases, dents, a fuzzy wing tip. |
| Brand test (cover the logo) | It could be any app's sticker pack. | It could be any Pop Mart-style brand. | Only MockMob: paper, the lamp and the visor. |
| Best use | Not needed. Its readability lesson is already in C. | Marketing and the shelf: "figure" shots, the unboxing feel, a real merch idea later. | The app: lobby, podium, reveal, stickers, share cards. |

**Recommendation: C for the app and for the cast.** Keep B as the marketing and merch treatment of the same characters (a Fold on its base for shelf tours, the exam-season drop poster, and a possible real toy one day). A is shelved.
Reasons:
1. The owner said the "Mobius paper family is a better direction". C is the paper family, and it sits beside Mobi and the subject art without a seam, so the whole app reads as one brand.
2. Gen Z's 2026 taste is moving away from slick, AI-smooth 3D towards things that look made (research 1.1). C's paper fibre and odd traits carry that. A's gloss is the look people now read as generated.
3. Genmoji's real lesson was readability at chat size, not the gloss. C keeps one bold silhouette, one colour and the visor's eyes, and it reads at 40 px on both grounds.
4. B is the best answer to "collectible toys are good, but I am not sure how we will be able to implement it". In the app, the shelf can show C Folds on a paper plinth, and B becomes the marketing photo style. Nobody has to build a toy line to get the collectible feeling.

Made in style C: 12 Folds (`cut/C-*.webp`), the Unfold reveal (`REVEAL-unfold.webp`) and the group shot with Mobi on the desk (`GROUP-mob-with-mobi.webp`). The cast sheet is `CAST-best.png`, at Night and Day.
Watch list (resolved in the final art, 19 Oct night, `characters/final/FINAL-ART.md`): Hop is redrawn as an unmistakable frog; Patang is a slim kite with visible sticks; Crane's visor is wide. Each Fold now has its own eyes inside the visor and one prop. ## 6. How the art goes into Flutter

- **Masters**: keep every 1024 PNG on flat black in `work/raw` (not shipped). Cut-outs are made with the subject-art matte (`work/matte2.py`: difference matte, linear-light downscale, clean edges on Night and Day).
- **Shipped sizes** (WebP, alpha, quality 90):
  - `fold-<name>-<pose>@512.webp` (long side 512 px): reveal, shelf detail, podium first place. About 40 to 70 KB.
  - `fold-<name>-<pose>@256.webp`: lobby seats, ghost markers, podium places, stickers. About 15 to 25 KB.
  - `fold-<name>-head@128.webp`: discs and chips at 24 to 40 dp, cropped from idle around the visor, not generated.
  - Budget for 12 Folds x 5 poses x 2 sizes plus heads: about 4 MB. Ship idle and cheer in the app bundle (about 1.4 MB) and download the rest the first time the Mob opens.
- **Poses per Fold** (same camera, framing and light as now): idle (standing, neutral, used most), cheer, think, shrug (the calm "not this one", never sad, per build spec 5.2), wave. The final pass generates all five for every Fold from its approved idle (`characters/final/FINAL-ART.md`).
- **Cheap animation, no Rive in build 1** (brand kit section 6, option C):
  - Squash and stretch on the whole still, pivoted at the boots: anticipation scaleY 0.94 and scaleX 1.04, then 1.06 and 0.96 on take-off, settle back with the existing spring under the 0.12 bounce cap.
  - Breathe: scaleY 1 to 1.028 from the feet on the 3600 ms sweep, staggered by member.
  - Blink: every Fold has the same visor, so a blink is one visor-coloured rounded rect that scales vertically over each eye for 120 ms. Store eye boxes per pose in `folds.json` (x, y, w, h for both eyes, in image fractions). Random 4 to 7 s, never in sync.
  - Sway: rotate about the boots by plus or minus 2 degrees on the 6400 ms sweep for lobby idles.
  - Parallax: three layers (the Fold, its contact shadow painted in code, the lamp pool) moving at 1, 0.5 and 0.25 with scroll or device tilt, capped at 8 dp.
  - Light: the lamp sweep and foil are a fragment shader over the still (foil only on Foil finishes). Glow ink is a colour-matrix plus a soft bloom on Night.
  - Pose change: cross-fade 120 ms on the squash low point, so it reads as one move.
  - Reduced motion: still frames and crossfades, no squash.
- **Finishes**: generate graph paper, glow ink and foil for the idle pose only (the shelf). In games the Fold shows plain, so the art count stays sane.
