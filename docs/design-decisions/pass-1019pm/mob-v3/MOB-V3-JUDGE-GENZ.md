# Mob v3 judge: as a 16-year-old and as the owner (19 Oct 2026, evening)

I judged Den Live and Den Arena, plus the Folds character world, on one question: would a 16-year-old in Delhi or Kota show this to friends, and would Atishay call it a 10/10?

**What I looked at:** every Night screen in both packs and both Day contact sheets, at full size for the den, ready, race, podium, chain, milestone, Free and Pro, and shelf screens. I also read the frame strips for both podiums, both reveals, both countdowns, both streak moments, Live's quest moment, Arena's go-live and ghost race, and the whole Arena motion sheet. On the character side I read STYLES-compare and CAST-best, the CHARACTER-WORLD lore and earn rules, and sections 1 to 5 and 10 to 12 of both specs. I also compared both against Den v2 S7, the screen he liked. I did not play the webm files frame by frame; the strips and the animated sheet were enough to judge timing and staging.

## Verdict in one paragraph

**Build Den Arena, and take five pieces from Den Live.** Arena feels more like a world. The den uses the app's own painted lamp-lit room, and in Day that room is sunlit and lovely. The race lanes still work with five friends. The podium handles ties honestly and opens with "Your Mob got 15 of 20 right together", which is the most shareable line in either pack. Arena also keeps the correct answer hidden until the room closes. Live has the better individual moments: the stage-lit podium that says "so far", the parcel that the character climbs out of, the "2/5 today" chain strip, the arc of ticks on the 14-day milestone with Mobi cheering, and the "Fix it together" answer sheet. Neither pack is 10/10 yet. **Arena scores 7.4 and Live 6.8.** The gap to 10 is the same for both:
- the characters are single-pose stills being shaken around;
- the question screen, where students spend about 70% of a game, is still a normal quiz with a strip on top;
- Free and Pro is a list, not a feeling;
- the earn rules contradict each other across the three documents.

## The characters (the Folds)

**What works:**
- **The story is right.** "Every mistake you fix leaves something behind" is the product's promise turned into a story. It is true, it is ours, and "Which Fold are you?" is a real Instagram and WhatsApp hook.
- **The cast feels Indian without being a costume.** Cutting the chai cup, Patang, Kandil and Naav, plus Dog-ear and Tally, are habits a student will recognise in a friend.
- **The family resemblance is what he liked.** He liked the "Mobius paper family", and the visor and capsule eyes deliver it.

**What a 16-year-old will say:**
- **They are all Mobi with a different body.** Every Fold has the same black visor, the same two white eyes, the same rubber arms and the same boots. Only Hop and Stamp smile, and only Kandil has its own eyelids. Labubu, Smiski and Sonny Angel are loved for their faces. Give each Fold its own eyes inside the visor (sleepy, squint, sparkle, worried, smug), and add one prop or accessory per Fold.
- **The three styles are nearly the same picture.** A, B and C differ by a gloss level and a base, so this is not a real choice to put in front of him. The real choice is range: idle, cheer, think, gentle-sad and wave per Fold, plus a blink.
- **Hop reads as a cat or a fox.** Redraw it with a wide frog mouth, eye bumps on top and long back legs. Do not ship a frog that looks like a cat.
- **Patang shares Mobi's diamond outline.** Give it a slimmer kite shape with visible sticks, and run the small-size check at 24 and 40 dp.
- **Crane's visor is too thin at small sizes.** Widen it.

**Style recommendation:**
- **C (lamp-lit paper) for the app.** It sits beside Mobi and the subject art with no seam.
- **B's base for collecting.** Show every shelf card and the end of every reveal with the Fold standing on a small paper base. The base is the signal Gen Z reads as "collectible figure", and it costs one extra layer, not a second art style. Use B renders for Reels, sticker packs and any printed merch.
- **Drop A.**

## What to build, and what to take from Live

Build Den Arena. Take these from Den Live:
1. **The podium stage:** the lamp narrows onto paper blocks and the label says "so far" while the 24-hour room is open. Keep Arena's collective headline, ties and fourth place inside it.
2. **The reveal:** the parcel wobbles, light leaks out and the Fold climbs out of the open parcel. Replace Arena's sunburst-and-swap.
3. **The chain strip:** daily paper ticks with today's live "2/5", and the 14-day milestone arc with Mobi cheering. Arena's ring chain looks generic next to it.
4. **The countdown:** paper tiles stamping 3, 2, 1, Go on the metronome beat. Put them over Arena's stopwatch so the hand sweeps while the numerals stamp.
5. **The social bits:** the floating preset sticker over its sender in the lobby, "Fix it together" as an answer sheet on the den wall with the Now-to-Wednesday path, and the "What your friend sees in WhatsApp" invite card.

Ideas neither pack has yet, needed for a 10/10:
- **Make the race visible while you answer.** Show preset reactions from friends ("Nice one", "So close") as small paper stickers on their lanes, a short hit-stop and stamp when anyone finishes, and a 3-in-a-row stamp that lands on your own lane.
- **Characters that react.** Your Fold leans in while you think, nods when you lock in, and on a wrong answer makes a calm shrug, never a sad face. This needs the pose set or Rive. Without it, every moment is a still being moved, and that is the main reason neither pack feels like a top game.
- **Make Pro feel like hosting.** A Pro member who starts a room gets a host moment: they switch the lamp on and everyone's Fold turns towards them. A whistle or bell sticker that only the host has would also work, as long as it is not a collectible. That gives a clear, desirable difference that changes nothing about marks or earning.
- **Fix the Free gate.** Free gets one sprint start per Mob per week, and Pro gets every format, live rooms, full mock together, insights and unlimited starts. That keeps a clear difference without a dead Mob (see the first fatal issue).

## Fatal, or must be fixed before build

1. **Pro-only start can leave the Mob with nothing to play.** While billing is off, nobody can start a game. After launch, most Mobs of four new students will have no Pro member, so all they can play is the weekly paper. Decide the billing-off rule now, and give Free a small start allowance (or at least the first game per Mob plus Pass the mic). "Free should not be completely restrictive" is his own line.
2. **Live leaks the answer.** During a 24-hour room, Live shows "Not this one. It was C" with the working. A friend who hasn't played can be told the answer, and the podium stops being fair. Use Arena's rule: you see your own right or wrong and +5 or -1, and the correct option and the explanation open when the room closes.
3. **Live's Free and Pro screen breaks two rules.**
   - It sells lamp themes through Pro on a screen that says nothing in the Mob is for sale. Arena earns the lamp theme at 12 Mob weeks; use that.
   - It shows a cheaper web price inside the Play app, which is a Google Play payments-policy risk. Keep price on the existing paywall, as Arena does.
4. **The earn rules contradict each other across the three documents.**
   - Kandil is a "first full mock" Fold in CHARACTER-WORLD but "this week's quest" in Arena.
   - Stamp needs 4 Mob weeks in CHARACTER-WORLD and Live, but 8 in Arena.
   - Foil Dart is "beat your habit" in CHARACTER-WORLD but "30 days in a row" in Arena.
   - CHARACTER-WORLD says no silhouettes; both shelves use them.
   - Both shelves let anyone pick any Fold, which makes collecting meaningless. Agree one table before anyone builds: four starters free, the rest earned, and you may sit as any Fold you have earned.
5. **The truth slips are small but real.**
   - Live's milestone says "You practised 14 days in a row, with one rest day". It was 13 practised and 1 rested; Arena's wording is right.
   - In Live's podium count-up, first place briefly shows fewer marks than second. Count up in step, or start from the final order.
   - Live's 14-day milestone (7:42 PM) and its 12-day chain (8:10 PM) contradict each other in one fixture.
6. **Arena's live room shows "Room code K7P 2M9" with "Send the code".** That is the Mob's invite code, so it invites people into the Mob rather than into the room. A live room should be Mob-only with no code. Separately, Arena says "the Mob never shows who has Pro", but only Pro can host, so the "Host" label shows it anyway. Drop the claim, or accept that hosting is visible.
7. **Nothing has run on a phone yet.** Ten-plus layered moments with large WebP stills need the emulator walk, the RAM rule and the low-end frame check before anyone promises "top game" motion.
