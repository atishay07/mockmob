# Verdict: the phone home page (council of 26 Nov 2026)

Sat: GPT (Codex CLI, ChatGPT subscription), Claude Opus (the reducer), Claude Opus (growth lead), Claude Sonnet (the
aspirant), Claude Haiku (the one who pays). Gemini did not sit: its CLI is installed and not signed in.

## Decision
Keep the live order and cut, never restructure. On the phone: remove the "works best on a laptop" note, everything
under the hero film except one line, the Statement section, and the Colleges strip as its own section (its logos move
into DU Compass). Daily Five and Sunday Mock become one calendar-page card, two rows, no openers, straight after the
Try five drill. Compass shows five colleges with "Show all 10". Pricing keeps the six pictures and loses its repeats.

## The count
All five ballots ranked the reducer's plan first (25 points of 25) and the growth lead's second (20). No split along
company lines: GPT's own position came last on every ballot, including its own, because it was the vaguest.

## Why (the arguments that carried)
- The clutter is repetition, counted from the page: six features named four times, a question answered twice, the price
  three times. Naming each thing once removes about a third of the page without touching a liked section. (all five)
- The first line a phone visitor reads is "MockMob works best on a laptop or desktop", and every sign-up is from a
  phone. (reducer, aspirant, growth, parent)
- Cuts in the live order are the kind of change the owner has accepted; restructures he has rejected. (reducer, GPT)

## Where the council overruled its own winner
- The reducer hid Daily Five in a "One place" tab. Every reviewer, the reducer included, called that the plan's weak
  point: a visible half-screen card after the drill turns "I got 3 of 5" into a reason to come back tomorrow.
- Pricing pictures: four positions wanted them dropped; at review three seats reversed, because once the hero grid is
  gone the pictures are the only list of features, and the owner prefers them.

## What the chair decided against the council, and why
- Compass slider: three seats said it must not play by itself. The owner asked for exactly that on 18 Nov (it plays and
  stops after 10 to 15 s). His word stands.
- Mistake Lab: the council wants four held screens on the phone, but split on which goes (Changes: reducer, growth;
  SWOT: aspirant, parent, growth at review). Not cut. It is a section he loves and the seats did not agree; his call.
- "One place: Practise" tab: three seats would cut it as a repeat of the drill. Kept: it is the only place the exam
  screen and NTA style are shown, and he has called that the most important screen.

## Dissent worth keeping
- The parent: "The slider shows 930 out of 1000 ... State the source or correct the scale before the page goes live."
- The growth lead: no funnel numbers exist, so every lift here is a hypothesis; first test is the drill's result card
  with and without "Save this score and get tomorrow's five. Sign up free."

## What would change it
Phone visitor data showing readers who see the feature grid or the Statement sign up more than those who do not.
