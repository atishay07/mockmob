## Answer shape for this brief (it replaces the usual shape)
VERDICT: one line, and a score out of 10 for the phone page as a whole.
Then at most seven lines, most serious first, each exactly:
ROAST: <a specific fault, and where on the page it is (name the section, or the sheet and screen)> | FIX: <what to change, concretely: the words, the size, the order, the CSS property and value, or the motion's duration and curve>
End with: KEEP: <the one thing on the page that is best and must not be touched>

A roast line without a fix is not allowed. Roast the work, never the people. Do not repeat what was already cut.
