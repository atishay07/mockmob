Partnership: admission of a partner

X and Y share profits in the ratio 3:2. Z is admitted for a 1/4 share, which he takes equally from X and Y. What is the new profit-sharing ratio?

A3:2:1
B9:6:5Your answer, −1
C19:11:10Correct
D11:19:10

Solution

Each old partner gives up 1/8. X keeps 3/5 − 1/8 = 19/40, Y keeps 2/5 − 1/8 = 11/40, and Z gets 1/4 = 10/40.
