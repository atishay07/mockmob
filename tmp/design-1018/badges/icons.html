One icon family

D8: "the icons in the UI can be improved". Both apps already use Lucide; the problem is the exceptions, the stroke drift and too many sizes.

1. The phone bar uses a second family

The app's bottom bar draws Material Rounded glyphs and swaps to a filled shape when active (bar_icons.dart). Every other icon in the app, and every web nav icon, is Lucide outline. Same tab, different picture on web and app.

Before: Material Rounded, filled when active
After: Lucide 24, stroke 1.75, active in accent with a bold label

2. Stroke drift

Glyph.jsx sets 1.75, but 44 web files import lucide-react directly and get 2.0; a few hand-set 2.3, 2.4 and 3. The app draws Lucide at its default weight.

Before: same icon, four weights in use
After: 1.75 everywhere from 16 to 24

3. Too many sizes

Web uses 13 icon sizes (10 to 40 px), the app 15 or more. Sizes 13, 15 and 17 sit next to 14, 16 and 18 and look like mistakes.

Before: sizes found in web code
After: three sizes

Before: 18 at 2.0 lead, 14 chevron
After: 20 at 1.75 lead in ink 2, 16 chevron in ink 3
